## 1. Senate Approves Important Bill to Speed Up Projects  


The US Senate recently passed a **key permitting reform bill** that aims to make it faster and easier to approve big projects like roads, bridges, and energy plants. This bill is important because it can help build things quicker and create jobs. It now moves to the House of Representatives for approval. Many people hope this bill will fix the slow process that often stops important projects. If the House agrees, it could lead to faster construction and better infrastructure. This is a big step for improving the country’s future. We will watch to see how the House responds to this bill.

## 2. Trump Administration Cut Many Legal Immigration Protections  


A report shows that during the Trump administration, many legal protections for immigrants were **stripped** or removed. This means some rules that helped immigrants stay in the US or get rights were taken away. The changes made it harder for some people to stay legally or get help. Immigration experts say this was a big shift in policy. Now, there is a strong **fight for immigration reform** to bring back or improve protections. Many groups hope the new government will fix these problems and create fairer laws. Immigration is a very important issue that affects millions of people.

> ### 영어 표현 학습

## 3. Russian Arrested for Spying on US Tech Company  


US authorities arrested a Russian man accused of **spying** on Palantir, a US defense technology company. Palantir works on software that helps the military and government with data and security. The spy was trying to get information about US support for Ukraine. This arrest shows how important technology and defense secrets are today. It also highlights ongoing tensions between the US and Russia. Protecting tech companies is very important to keep national security safe. The case will continue as investigators learn more.

## 4. US Labor Market Shows Early Signs of Recovery  


The US job market is showing signs of **revival** as more job postings appear. This means businesses are looking to hire more workers again after some slow months. The recovery is good news for people searching for jobs. However, many workers still worry about inflation, which is **eroding** their buying power. Inflation means prices go up, so money does not go as far as before. Experts say if the job market keeps growing, it could help the economy. But challenges like inflation remain a concern for many Americans.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **signs of revival** — 회복의 조짐  
>   _The economy shows **signs of revival** after a slow period._  
> - **job postings appear** — 구인 공고가 나타나다  
>   _More **job postings appear** as companies hire._  
> - **eroding buying power** — 구매력을 약화시키다  
>   _Inflation is **eroding buying power** for many people._  
> - **keep growing** — 계속 성장하다  
>   _If the job market **keeps growing**, the economy will improve._
