## 1. Republicans Blame Iran War Talk for Election Losses 

The Republican Party lost many seats in the 2026 midterm elections. One Republican leader, Vance, **broke with** former President Trump by saying the talk about going to war with Iran hurt their chances. He believes this war talk made many voters worried and caused them to vote for other parties. This is important because it shows a difference inside the Republican Party about what ideas work best in elections. The midterm results will affect the next years of US politics and policies. People will watch to see if the party changes its message or stays the same. This story shows how foreign policy discussions can **play a big role** in domestic elections.

How the government handles immigration will affect millions of people living in the U.S., including workers and families. We will see if there are new policies that make immigration easier or harder. In September, the number of job postings in the U.S. increased a lot. This means many companies want to hire more workers soon. Experts think this could show the job market is getting stronger after some slow months. More job openings usually help people find work and improve the economy. Also, layoffs are at their lowest in a year and a half, which is a good sign. This rise in hiring could continue if the economy stays stable. It shows how the job market **can change quickly** depending on many factors.

## 4. FBI Arrest Sparks Fear of Spies in U.S. Tech Companies 

The FBI recently arrested a person who is accused of spying for a foreign country inside a U.S. tech company. This case has raised worries that some tech companies might be hiring spies without knowing it. The tech sector is very important for the economy and national security. If spies work inside these companies, they can steal secrets or cause damage. This arrest shows how the government is trying to protect American technology. It also reminds companies to be careful about who they hire. The story highlights the risks of **foreign spying** in the technology world.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **spying for** — ~를 위해 스파이를 하다  
>   _The agent was caught **spying for** another country._  
> - **raise worries** — 걱정을 불러일으키다  
>   _The news **raised worries** about safety in the city._  
> - **national security** — 국가 안보  
>   _Protecting **national security** is a government priority._  
> - **be careful about** — ~에 대해 조심하다  
>   _You should **be careful about** sharing personal information online._
