## 1. Searchers Find Debris of Missing Medical Plane Near Nantucket  


A medical plane flying to Boston went missing near Nantucket recently. Search teams have now found parts of the plane in the ocean. This discovery is important because it helps rescue workers understand what happened. The plane was carrying urgent medical supplies and people were worried about those on board. Finding debris means the search can focus on the area to locate survivors or the rest of the aircraft. This event shows how dangerous flying over the ocean can be. Authorities will keep investigating to learn the cause of the crash and how to prevent it in the future. The families affected are hoping for good news soon.

## 2. Supreme Court to Review Trump’s Immigration Detention Policy  


The U.S. Supreme Court will soon decide on an important immigration case. The case is about a policy that requires immigrants to be held in detention while their cases are processed. This policy was made during the Trump administration and has caused a lot of debate. Supporters say it helps control immigration and keeps people from disappearing. Critics say it violates immigrants’ rights and can cause harm. The Supreme Court’s decision will affect many immigrants and how the government handles immigration. This case shows the ongoing struggle to balance safety and fairness in immigration laws.

## 3. US Labor Market Remains Steady Despite Slower Job Growth  


In September, the U.S. job market did not grow as fast as expected, but it stayed steady overall. Employers added fewer jobs than experts predicted, which surprised some people. Even so, the labor market is still strong and many people are working. This steady growth means the economy is not growing too fast or too slow. It also suggests that the Federal Reserve may keep its interest rates stable for now. Workers may find it a bit harder to get new jobs, but the market is not in trouble. This news is important for anyone looking for work or watching the economy closely.

## 4. Silicon Valley Faces Hiring Challenges as H-1B Fees Could Rise  


The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has proposed a big increase in the fees for H-1B visas. These visas allow tech companies to hire skilled workers from other countries. The proposed fee could rise to over $100,000. Many Silicon Valley companies say this will make it much harder to **hire top talent**. They worry the high cost will stop them from bringing in the best workers they need. This is a big issue because the tech industry depends on skilled immigrants to grow and innovate. The government will review the proposal, but the tech world is already concerned about the future of hiring.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **hire top talent** — 최고의 인재를 고용하다  
>   _Tech companies want to **hire top talent** from around the world._  
> - **proposed fee** — 제안된 수수료  
>   _The **proposed fee** for H-1B visas is much higher than before._  
> - **skilled workers** — 숙련된 노동자  
>   _Many companies rely on **skilled workers** to stay competitive._  
> - **make it harder** — 어렵게 만들다  
>   _The new fee could **make it harder** to attract foreign employees._
