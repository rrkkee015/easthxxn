## 1. Protesters Interrupt Trump During Texas Senate Rally 

At a recent political rally in Texas, former President Trump faced interruptions from protesters. He was there to support a candidate running for the U.S. Senate. The protesters showed their disagreement loudly, making it hard for Trump to speak. This event shows how divided opinions are in some parts of the country. It also highlights how political events can quickly become tense. The candidates and their supporters want to **rally** voters to join their side before the election. This moment might affect how people feel about the candidates and the upcoming vote. It shows how important public support is in elections.

> - **rally** — 지지자들을 모으다, 결집시키다  
>   _The community will **rally** around the cause to make a change._  
> - **interrupt** — 방해하다, 중단시키다

> - **supporter** — 지지자

## 2. Mass Deportations Are Breaking Democracy 

The American Civil Liberties Union says that mass deportations are harming democracy in the United States. Mass deportations mean sending large numbers of immigrants out of the country. The group argues that this practice breaks families and hurts communities. Many people feel confused and scared about their future. The ACLU wants new laws to protect immigrant families and stop forced separations. They believe democracy is about fairness and protecting all people’s rights. If changes happen, it could make immigration policies more humane and just.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **mass deportations** — 대규모 추방  
>   _The country saw an increase in **mass deportations** last year._  
> - **democracy** — 민주주의  
>   _In a **democracy**, people have the right to vote._  
> - **break families** — 가족을 헤어지게 하다  
>   _Strict immigration rules sometimes **break families** apart._  
> - **protect** — 보호하다  
>   _Laws should **protect** the rights of all citizens._

## 3. September Hiring Shows Labor Market is Steady 

In September, the U.S. continued to add jobs, but the growth was slower than before. Experts say the labor market is still on a **steady track**, meaning it is stable and not changing too fast. This is important because it shows the economy is not losing strength quickly. Many companies are still hiring workers, but some are cautious because of uncertainty about the future. This steady hiring helps people find jobs but also shows that the economy might slow down soon. With midterm elections coming, this economic stability could affect voters’ opinions.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **steady track** — 안정적인 경로  
>   _The company's sales are on a **steady track** this year._  
> - **labor market** — 노동시장  
>   _The **labor market** is recovering after the economic slowdown._  
> - **growth** — 성장, 증가  
>   _There was strong **growth** in the tech sector last quarter._  
> - **uncertainty** — 불확실성  
>   _Economic **uncertainty** makes businesses hesitate to invest._

## 4. America’s Tech Scene Grows Beyond Silicon Valley 

The tech industry in the United States is no longer just in Silicon Valley. New tech hubs are growing in other cities across the country. This expansion means more jobs and opportunities in technology for people outside California. Many companies are moving or opening offices in places with lower costs and talented workers. This change helps the tech industry **expand** and become more diverse. It also means more cities can benefit from technology and innovation. Experts think this trend will continue as tech keeps growing nationwide.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **expand** — 확장하다  
>   _The company plans to **expand** its business in Asia._  
> - **tech hub** — 기술 중심지, IT 중심지  
>   _New **tech hubs** are emerging in cities like Austin and Denver._  
> - **opportunity** — 기회  
>   _This job fair offers many **opportunities** for young professionals._  
> - **innovation** — 혁신  
>   _Technology drives **innovation** in many industries._
