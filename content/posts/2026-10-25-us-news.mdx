## 1. Protests Spread Across France as Police Try to Stop Riots  


Several cities in France are seeing large protests as people march on the streets. The protests began because of political and social issues that many citizens feel strongly about. Police are working hard to **prevent** more violent riots from happening during these marches. The situation is tense because some protesters have clashed with police in the past. These demonstrations are important because they show how people in France want their voices heard. The government is watching closely to see if the protests will calm down or grow even bigger. How this situation ends could affect the political future of France.

Healthcare providers in New York are facing new problems because of changes in immigration rules. Many doctors and nurses come from other countries, and these rules affect who can work legally in the U.S. Some healthcare workers worry about their future and their ability to help patients. The changes may cause a shortage of workers in hospitals and clinics. This is a big concern because it could affect the quality of healthcare for many people. Officials are trying to find solutions, but the situation remains difficult. Understanding these challenges helps us see how immigration policies affect everyday life.

## 3. U.S. Job Market Growth Slows, Reducing Pressure on Federal Reserve  


The number of new jobs in the U.S. is growing more slowly this year. This slower job growth means less pressure on the Federal Reserve, which controls interest rates. When the job market grows too fast, the Fed may raise interest rates to control inflation. But now, the Fed might wait longer before making any changes. This news affects many people because interest rates influence loans and mortgages. Economists watch these trends carefully to predict what will happen next in the economy. For recent college graduates, this slower growth may mean fewer job opportunities at first.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job growth** — 일자리 증가  
>   _The **job growth** rate is slower than last year._  
> - **pressure** — 압박, 부담  
>   _There is less **pressure** on the Fed to raise rates._  
> - **interest rates** — 이자율  
>   _Higher **interest rates** make loans more expensive._  
> - **forecast** — 예측하다, 예측  
>   _Experts **forecast** slower economic growth this year._

Tech companies work with important information and advanced technology, so security is very important. The government is increasing efforts to protect these companies from spying. This issue shows the risks that come with the growth of technology and global business. Many people are watching to see how companies will improve their security in response.
