## 1. Why Voting in the Midterms Matters for Refugees and Immigrants  
  

Voting in the midterm elections is very important, especially for refugees and immigrants living in the US. These elections decide who will represent people in Congress and other government jobs for the next two years. Many laws that affect immigrants’ lives, like healthcare and education, can change depending on who wins. Refugees and immigrants often feel their voices are not heard, so voting helps them be part of the decision-making process. Groups are working hard to encourage these communities to vote because their participation can really **shape the future**. The midterms will show if politicians listen to these groups’ needs. This election can also affect policies about immigration and support for newcomers.

## 2. US Job Growth Slowed Sharply in September  
  

The US job market grew much slower in September than before. Only 33,000 new jobs were added last month, which is much less than usual. Experts say this is a sign the economy might be cooling down. The unemployment rate went up a little to 4.2%, meaning more people are looking for work. Even though job openings are still high, companies may be hiring fewer workers because of worries about the future. This slower growth could affect workers and businesses if it continues. Many people watch these numbers closely because they show how healthy the economy is. The government and companies may make changes to help create more jobs soon.

## 3. FBI Arrest Revives Fears of Spies in U.S. Tech Companies  
  

The FBI recently arrested a person accused of spying for a foreign government inside a US tech company. This arrest has made many people worried that spies could be working in important technology firms. These companies develop new ideas and products that are very valuable to the country. If spies steal secrets, it could harm US security and business. The FBI is working hard to find and stop these threats. The tech industry is also trying to improve security and check who they hire. This case shows why protecting sensitive information in technology is very important today.

## 4. Judge Criticizes Trump Administration’s Plan to Change DC Golf Course  
  

A judge recently criticized the Trump administration’s plan to make big changes to a golf course in Washington, DC. The plan was to overhaul the golf course, but the judge said the government did not follow the right legal steps. The decision means the project cannot move forward until the problems are fixed. This golf course is important because it is on public land and has historical value. Local groups and citizens were concerned about the changes and the impact on the environment. This case shows how courts can stop government plans if they do not follow the law. The administration may have to rethink its approach and try again.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **criticize** — 비판하다  
>   _The judge **criticized** the plan for not following the rules._  
> - **overhaul** — 전면 개편하다  
>   _They wanted to **overhaul** the golf course._  
> - **move forward** — 진행하다  
>   _The project cannot **move forward** yet._  
> - **follow legal steps** — 법적 절차를 따르다  
>   _It is important to **follow legal steps** in projects._
