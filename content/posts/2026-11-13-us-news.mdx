## 1. Gunman Blames Building Problems for Violent Attack 

A recent attack in Washington D.C. has been linked to the gunman’s **frustrations** with poor building maintenance. The man involved said that the bad conditions in his apartment building made him angry. This anger grew over time and may have played a role in why he acted violently. Experts say this shows how small problems in daily life can sometimes lead to big consequences. The authorities are now looking more closely at building safety and tenant complaints. This story reminds us how important it is to fix problems before they get worse. It might lead to better rules to protect residents in the future.

## 3. U.S. Job Market Shows Slow but Steady Growth 

The latest U.S. jobs report shows the economy added 165,000 jobs in October. The unemployment rate stayed the same at 4.1%. Experts describe the job market as **low-hire, low-fire**, meaning companies are not hiring or firing many workers. This steady trend suggests employers are cautious but still growing their teams slowly. The technology sector, in particular, is adding jobs but at a modest pace. This kind of job market means workers have some security but may not see big new opportunities right now. Many people hope this trend will continue with steady growth.

> - **job market** — 취업 시장

> - **low-hire, low-fire** — 적은 채용과 해고

> - **steady growth** — 꾸준한 성장

> - **at a modest pace** — 적당한 속도로

## 4. Silicon Valley Faces Challenges After Nvidia CEO’s AI Exit 

Silicon Valley is reacting strongly after Nvidia’s CEO Jensen Huang announced he will step away from his AI leadership role. Nvidia is a major company in artificial intelligence technology. Huang’s exit leaves a big question about the future of AI development in the U.S. At the same time, news reports reveal concerns about Chinese spies in Silicon Valley trying to steal technology secrets. These two stories together show how the tech industry faces both leadership changes and security risks. Many people wonder how these challenges will affect America’s position in global technology. The coming months will be important for the future of AI and tech security.
