## 1. Court Blocks Trump's Board from Demolishing Kennedy Center Without Warning  
  

A judge has ruled that former President Trump’s board cannot **demolish** the Kennedy Center without giving **advance notice**. The Kennedy Center is a famous performing arts venue in Washington, D.C. The board wanted to make big changes, but the court said they must **warn people ahead of time**. This ruling is important because it protects a historic place from sudden destruction. It also shows how the legal system can stop decisions that might harm public spaces. The board will likely need to follow official rules before making any changes. This case may continue as both sides prepare for more legal steps.

The Trump administration has faced two major **legal setbacks** related to its immigration policies. Courts have blocked parts of policies that tried to limit immigrants and asylum seekers. These decisions are important because they affect many people who want to enter or stay in the U.S. The setbacks show that the government must follow laws and respect people’s rights. Immigration remains a hot topic with strong opinions on both sides. These court rulings could change how immigration is handled in the future. Supporters of immigration reform are watching closely to see what happens next.

## 3. US Job Market Shows Strong Growth with 162,000 New Jobs in August  
  

The US added 162,000 jobs in August, showing that the job market is **picking up** again. This growth is important because it affects the economy and inflation. When more people work, they spend money, which helps businesses. However, more jobs can also cause prices to rise, which worries some experts. The data shows that hiring is strong in many industries like healthcare and construction. This hiring boost puts more focus on inflation and how the government will respond. Watching these numbers helps us understand the country’s economic health.

## 4. America’s Tech Hubs Are Growing Outside Silicon Valley  
  

America’s technology industry is no longer only in Silicon Valley. New tech hubs are **expanding** in cities across the country. Places like Austin, Miami, and Denver are attracting tech companies and workers. This change is because of lower costs and better quality of life outside California. The shift means more opportunities for people in different regions. It also shows how tech is becoming more spread out and less focused on one place. This trend could help the US stay a leader in technology by using more diverse talent.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **tech hub** — 기술 중심지  
>   _Seattle is a growing **tech hub** with many software companies._  
> - **expanding** — 확장하는, 넓히는  
>   _The company is **expanding** its offices to new cities._  
> - **quality of life** — 삶의 질  
>   _People move to smaller towns for a better **quality of life**._
