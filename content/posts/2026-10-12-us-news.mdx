## 1. Tense Debate Shakes Up South Carolina Senate Race  


The only debate for the South Carolina Senate race became **testy** as candidates sharply disagreed on many issues. This debate is very important because it helps voters understand each candidate’s views before the election. Both sides tried to **make their points** clearly, but the discussion got heated at times. The debate showed how divided people are about the future of the state and the country. Some experts say this could affect how people vote in November. The candidates focused on topics like the economy, immigration, and national security. Voters will watch carefully to see who came out stronger in this important **political battle**.

## 2. Immigration Policies Are Raising Food Prices for Farmers  


Farmers in the US say that current immigration policies are making food more expensive. Many farms depend on immigrant workers to plant, tend, and harvest crops. Because of strict rules and fewer workers allowed, farmers face higher costs or not enough help. This means prices for fruits, vegetables, and other foods are going up for everyone. Farmers warn this problem could get worse if immigration rules stay tight. Some experts believe that changing immigration policies could help lower food prices and support farmers. This issue shows how immigration affects everyday life in many ways.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **immigration policies** — 이민 정책  
>   _The government changed its **immigration policies** recently._  
> - **making food more expensive** — 음식값을 올리다  
>   _New rules are **making food more expensive** for consumers._  
> - **fewer workers allowed** — 허용되는 일꾼 수가 적다  
>   _There are **fewer workers allowed** on farms now._

## 3. US Job Market Shows Weak Growth in September  


In September, the US added only 29,000 new jobs, which is a much smaller number than usual. Experts say the job market is losing its **spark** or energy to grow fast. Many companies are hiring fewer workers because of economic worries and other problems. Even though the economy is still growing, job growth is slow and people may find it harder to get new jobs soon. Some believe that this slow hiring could affect how much money people earn and how confident they feel about their future. The government and businesses will watch these numbers closely in the coming months. It’s important to see if the job market will get stronger or stay weak.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job market** — 일자리 시장  
>   _The **job market** is very competitive nowadays._  
> - **lacks spark** — 활기가 없다  
>   _The economy **lacks spark** this month._  
> - **slow hiring** — 느린 채용  
>   _Companies are doing **slow hiring** because of uncertainty._

## 4. Silicon Valley AI Founders Support Industry Self-Policing on Safety  


In Silicon Valley, leaders in artificial intelligence (AI) are supporting President Trump’s idea that the tech industry should **police itself** when it comes to safety. This means companies would watch their own work to make sure AI is safe, instead of only relying on government rules. Many AI founders believe this approach will help the industry grow faster while still protecting people. However, some experts worry that self-policing may not be enough to stop problems with AI. The debate shows how important AI safety has become as the technology spreads quickly. What happens next could change how AI is developed around the world.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **police itself** — 스스로 감시하다, 통제하다  
>   _The company decided to **police itself** to avoid mistakes._  
> - **artificial intelligence (AI)** — 인공지능  
>   _**Artificial intelligence** is changing many industries._  
> - **grow faster** — 더 빨리 성장하다  
>   _The tech sector wants to **grow faster** without extra rules._
