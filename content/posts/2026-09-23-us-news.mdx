## 1. U.S. to Build Two New Military Bases in Greenland 

The United States has agreed to build two new military bases in Greenland. This deal is part of a new agreement with Denmark, which controls Greenland. The U.S. wants to **strengthen its presence** in the Arctic region because of its strategic importance. These bases will help monitor activities in the area and improve defense capabilities. Greenland is close to Russia and North America, making it very important for security. Building these bases will take time, but it shows that the U.S. is serious about protecting its interests in the Arctic. This move might increase tensions with other countries watching the region closely.

## 2. DHS Proposes Ending the 60-Day H-1B Grace Period 

The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has suggested a new rule to end the 60-day grace period for H-1B visa holders. This grace period lets people stay in the U.S. for 60 days after their job ends. The new rule would mean they must leave or find a new job faster. The government says this change will **strengthen immigration control** and reduce illegal work. However, many workers and companies worry that it will make life harder for skilled immigrants. This rule, if approved, will affect thousands of workers who rely on the grace period to search for new jobs or prepare to leave the country.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **grace period** — 유예 기간  
>   _Students get a **grace period** of two weeks to submit late assignments._  
> - **strengthen immigration control** — 이민 통제를 강화하다  
>   _The government plans to **strengthen immigration control** at the borders._  
> - **if approved** — 승인되면  
>   _The new policy will take effect **if approved** by Congress._

## 3. U.S. Job Growth Shows Mixed Results in 2026 

Job growth in the U.S. during 2026 has been slower than many experts hoped. New reports show that while some months had strong hiring, overall growth is below **optimal levels**. In August, payrolls rose by 162,000 jobs, which was better than expected. The unemployment rate is now at 4.1%, a sign the job market is fairly strong but not perfect. Economists say this slow growth might affect how the government handles inflation and interest rates. The job market is important because it affects people’s incomes and the health of the economy. We will watch closely to see if job growth improves in the coming months.
