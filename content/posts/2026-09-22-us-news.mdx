## 1. ICE Agent Shoots Man After Car is Struck Twice 

A man said he was shot by an ICE agent after his car was hit two times during a stop. The incident has caused concern and questions about how the situation was handled. The man’s story has drawn attention because it is unusual for law enforcement to use such force. ICE is the U.S. agency that handles immigration enforcement. This case might lead to investigations and calls for changes in how agents act during arrests. Many people worry about safety and fairness when officers use guns. The outcome could affect how immigration laws are enforced in the future.

## 2. Trump’s Immigration Policy Reminds Us of the 1920s 

Experts say that former President Trump’s immigration policies are similar to those in the 1920s. During that time, the U.S. limited certain groups of immigrants from entering the country. This history helps us understand today’s debates about who should be allowed to come to America. The 1920s laws were strict and based on ideas about race and nationality. Trump’s policies also focused on reducing immigration from specific places. Learning about the past shows us why immigration is still a difficult issue in the U.S. It may also help lawmakers make better decisions in the future.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **remind us of** — 우리에게 ~을 상기시키다  
>   _The policy **reminds us of** old immigration laws._  
> - **limit** — 제한하다  
>   _The government decided to **limit** the number of newcomers._  
> - **debate** — 토론, 논쟁  
>   _There is a big **debate** about immigration policy._  
> - **based on** — ~에 근거하여  
>   _The laws were **based on** certain ideas about race._  
> - **make better decisions** — 더 나은 결정을 내리다  
>   _History can help us **make better decisions** today._

## 3. America’s Tech Scene Moves Beyond Silicon Valley 

The U.S. technology industry is growing in places outside Silicon Valley. For many years, Silicon Valley was the main area for tech companies and innovation. Now, cities in other states are becoming new centers for technology. This change is good because it spreads jobs and opportunities more evenly. More people can find work in tech without moving to California. It also helps businesses grow in different parts of the country. Experts say this trend will continue and might change how the tech world looks in the future.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **move beyond** — ~을 넘어서다, ~이외로 확장되다  
>   _Tech companies are **moving beyond** Silicon Valley._  
> - **main area** — 주요 지역  
>   _Silicon Valley was the **main area** for tech jobs._  
> - **spread opportunities** — 기회를 퍼뜨리다  
>   _New tech hubs help **spread opportunities** across the country._  
> - **trend** — 경향, 추세  
>   _The **trend** of tech growth outside California is strong._  
> - **change how ... looks** — ~의 모습을 바꾸다  
>   _This could **change how** the tech industry **looks** soon._

## 4. August Jobs Report Shows Strong Hiring Growth 

In August, the U.S. added 162,000 new jobs, more than many experts expected. This strong hiring shows that the job market is healthy. The unemployment rate stayed low at 4.1%. When more people have jobs, it can affect other parts of the economy, like prices of goods. The report makes the government focus again on controlling inflation, which means rising prices. Some think the Federal Reserve might adjust interest rates because of this news. Overall, the job market’s strength is good for workers but brings challenges for the economy.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **add jobs** — 일자리를 추가하다, 늘리다  
>   _The country **added jobs** faster than expected._  
> - **job market** — 노동 시장, 취업 시장  
>   _The **job market** is strong this year._  
> - **unemployment rate** — 실업률  
>   _The **unemployment rate** stayed at 4.1%._  
> - **focus on** — ~에 집중하다  
>   _The government will **focus on** inflation now._  
> - **adjust interest rates** — 금리를 조정하다  
>   _The Federal Reserve might **adjust interest rates** soon._
