## 1. Rising Tensions Over Boycott of Jewish-Owned Restaurants in Philadelphia  


In Philadelphia, tensions are growing because of a boycott against Jewish-owned restaurants. Some groups want to stop supporting these businesses to protest political reasons. This has caused strong reactions from people who see the boycott as unfair or harmful. The issue is dividing the community and raising concerns about discrimination. Local leaders are trying to find a way to calm the situation and bring people together. This conflict shows how political actions can affect everyday life and businesses. It is important because it affects community trust and freedom of choice. How this will develop depends on whether dialogue can replace conflict in the city.

## 2. Trump Says Immigration Policy Was ‘Too Tough’  


Former President Trump told a TV show that his immigration policies were **too tough**. He said the strict deportation rules may have hurt his party in the recent elections. Trump seemed to admit that the hard approach did not work well politically. Immigration is a big topic in the U.S. because it affects many people and communities. Changes in policy can influence how the country controls its borders and treats immigrants. Experts think this new statement might lead to softer immigration rules in the future. It shows how leaders sometimes rethink their decisions after seeing the results.

## 3. US Job Growth Shows Strength Despite Economic Concerns

Experts say this is a good sign for the economy’s health. However, some uncertainties remain, like inflation and global issues. The report helps government leaders decide how to manage future policies. Overall, the job market looks hopeful for now, giving confidence to workers and businesses alike.

## 4. Silicon Valley Tech Giants Adjust Strategy After US Midterms  


After the US midterm elections, big tech companies in Silicon Valley are changing their lobbying strategies. They had previously shown support for President Trump, but now they face pressure from both political parties. This change means tech leaders want to stay on good terms with whoever is in power. Lobbying is how companies try to influence government decisions to benefit their business. The tech industry is very important for the economy and innovation. These new strategies may affect future laws about technology, privacy, and business. Silicon Valley’s moves show how politics and business are closely connected.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **lobbying strategy** — 로비 전략  
>   _The company changed its **lobbying strategy** to gain support._  
> - **showed support** — 지지를 보였다  
>   _Several leaders **showed support** for the new plan._  
> - **stay on good terms** — 좋은 관계를 유지하다  
>   _It’s important to **stay on good terms** with business partners._  
> - **closely connected** — 밀접하게 연결된  
>   _Politics and business are **closely connected** in many cases._
