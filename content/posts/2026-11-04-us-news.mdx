## 1. Democrats Win Back the House as Trump’s Approval Hits a Low 

In the recent 2026 House elections, the Democratic Party won the majority of seats. This means they will control the House of Representatives. Many experts say this result shows a clear message from American voters. At the same time, former President Donald Trump's approval rating fell to a historic low. This means fewer people support his ideas and leadership now. The House majority win is important because it affects how laws will be made in the next years. The Democrats plan to focus on issues like healthcare and the economy. This election may change the political direction of the country.

## 2. How Trump Changed U.S. Immigration Rules 

Donald Trump’s time as president brought many changes to U.S. immigration policies. His government made it harder for some people to enter or stay in the country. For example, some visa programs were limited, and border rules were tightened. These decisions affected millions of immigrants and their families. Supporters say these changes improved security, but critics say they caused problems for families and workers. Understanding these changes helps us see how immigration debates continue in America. Some rules may stay, while others could change again in the future.

## 3. US Job Postings Jump in September, Showing Hiring May Increase 

In September, the number of job postings in the U.S. went up sharply. This means more companies are looking to hire workers soon. Experts think this is a good sign for people searching for jobs. Also, layoffs are at their lowest level in one and a half years, which means fewer people are losing work. This increase in job openings may help the economy grow and give workers more choices. However, it is important to watch if these trends continue in the coming months. Many people are hopeful that the job market will get stronger.

Two 18-year-old Indian American entrepreneurs decided not to follow the usual engineering college path in India. Instead, they moved to Silicon Valley and raised $1.3 million to start their own business. This is a big achievement for such young people. It shows how new ideas and courage can open doors in the tech world. Their success story inspires many young people who want to create their own opportunities. Silicon Valley is known as a place where startups can grow quickly with the right support and money.
