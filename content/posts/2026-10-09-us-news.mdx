## 1. ICE Agent Shoots Man in New York City Neighborhood 

A man was shot by an ICE agent in Marble Hill, a neighborhood in New York City. Authorities say the incident happened during an ICE operation. ICE stands for Immigration and Customs Enforcement, a U.S. agency that handles immigration law enforcement. The shooting raised questions about the use of force by ICE officers. Many people are watching this case closely because it affects trust in law enforcement. The police are investigating the shooting to find out exactly what happened. The community is concerned about safety and fairness in how ICE carries out its work.

## 2. Highlights from the US Senate Debate in Michigan 

Two candidates debated for a U.S. Senate seat in Michigan. Their names are El-Sayed and Rogers. The debate covered many important topics like the economy, healthcare, and education. Voters are listening carefully because this election will decide who represents Michigan in the Senate. Both candidates shared different ideas on how to improve life for people in the state. ## 3. US Construction Employment Grows in September 

In September, the construction industry added 11,000 new jobs. This shows that construction hiring is still going well, especially for buildings that are not homes. Construction jobs are important because they help build schools, offices, and other places people need. Even though some parts of the economy are slow, construction work is steady. This is good news for workers and the economy. The growth means more people have work and can support their families. Experts will watch next month to see if the trend continues.

## 4. Silicon Valley Protests Huge H-1B Visa Fee Increase 

The U.S. Department of Homeland Security wants to raise the H-1B visa fee to over $100,000. The H-1B visa allows companies to hire skilled workers from other countries. Many Silicon Valley companies say this fee increase is too high. They ask, "**how will we hire top talent** if fees become so expensive?" The tech industry depends on these visas to bring in experts who help create new technology. If the fee goes up, it could slow down hiring and innovation. The government is still deciding if they will approve the new fee.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **H-1B visa** — H-1B 비자 (전문직 취업 비자)  
>   _Many tech workers come to the U.S. on an **H-1B visa**._  
> - **fee increase** — 수수료 인상  
>   _The company is unhappy about the **fee increase**._  
> - **top talent** — 최고의 인재  
>   _Silicon Valley wants to hire the best **top talent**._  
> - **depends on** — ~에 의존하다  
>   _The industry **depends on** skilled workers._  
> - **slow down** — 늦추다, 지연시키다  
>   _The fee hike could **slow down** hiring._
