## 1. Kennedy Center Closes Amid Political Dispute 

The Kennedy Center, a famous arts venue in Washington, D.C., has closed because of a **naming fight**. The dispute involves former President Trump, who has threatened to stop important repairs to the center. The problem started when there was a decision to name part of the center after someone Trump disagrees with. This fight has put the repairs on hold, causing the center to close temporarily. The closure affects many performers and visitors who planned to attend shows. This issue shows how political disagreements can **spill over** into cultural places. People hope a solution will come soon to reopen the center and continue its work.

## 2. Trump Administration Faces Legal Setbacks on Immigration 

The Trump administration has faced two important court defeats about immigration rules. These legal setbacks stopped some of the administration’s policies from moving forward. The courts said that the government did not follow the proper steps before changing the immigration laws. This is important because these policies affect thousands of immigrants trying to enter or stay in the U.S. The decisions show the power of the legal system to **push back** against government actions. Immigration is a very sensitive topic, and the court rulings may change what happens next. Many people are watching closely to see how the government will respond.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **legal setback** — 법적인 차질, 실패  
>   _The company faced a **legal setback** after losing the court case._  
> - **move forward** — 진행하다, 앞으로 나아가다  
>   _The project cannot **move forward** without approval._  
> - **proper steps** — 적절한 절차  
>   _You must follow the **proper steps** when applying for a visa._  
> - **push back** — 반대하다, 저항하다  
>   _The community decided to **push back** against the new policy._  
> - **sensitive topic** — 민감한 주제  
>   _Immigration is a **sensitive topic** for many people._

## 3. Strong Job Growth Brings Focus Back to Inflation 

In August, the U.S. added 162,000 new jobs, showing a strong hiring **burst**. This number surprised some experts because it was higher than expected. When more people get jobs, they have more money to spend. This can make prices go up, which is called inflation. Because of this job growth, many economists say inflation will stay an important issue. The government and the Federal Reserve will watch these numbers carefully. They may decide to change their policies to control inflation if the economy keeps growing fast.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job growth** — 일자리 증가  
>   _The city saw steady **job growth** last year._  
> - **burst** — 갑작스러운 증가  
>   _There was a hiring **burst** after the company opened a new office._  
> - **higher than expected** — 예상보다 높은  
>   _Sales were **higher than expected** this quarter._  
> - **inflation** — 물가 상승  
>   _High **inflation** means prices for goods and services increase._  
> - **keep an eye on** — 주의를 기울이다, 지켜보다  
>   _Investors **keep an eye on** the stock market trends._

## 4. America’s Tech Hubs Grow Beyond Silicon Valley 

The U.S. tech industry is no longer focused only in Silicon Valley. Many cities across the country are becoming important tech hubs. Places like Austin, Miami, and Denver are attracting tech companies and workers. This expansion helps spread job opportunities and innovation beyond California. Experts say this change is good for the economy and can reduce the high costs of living in Silicon Valley. It also creates new communities of tech professionals. As a result, the tech scene in America is growing wider and more diverse.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **tech hub** — 기술 중심지  
>   _Seattle is a growing **tech hub** in the northwest._  
> - **spread job opportunities** — 일자리 기회를 넓히다  
>   _New factories will **spread job opportunities** in small towns._  
> - **innovation** — 혁신  
>   _The company is known for its focus on **innovation**._  
> - **high costs of living** — 높은 생활비  
>   _Many people move to cheaper cities to avoid **high costs of living**._  
> - **more diverse** — 더 다양한  
>   _The city’s population is becoming **more diverse** each year._
