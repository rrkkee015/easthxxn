## 1. Historic Military Execution Set for Fort Hood Shooter 

The US military is preparing to execute a soldier who committed a serious crime at Fort Hood by firing squad. This will be the first time since World War Two that the military uses this method. The case is important because the firing squad is a rare and old form of punishment in the US. The military wants to send a strong message about justice and discipline. Some people support this decision, while others think it is too harsh. The execution is planned to happen soon, but it might face legal challenges. This story shows how the military handles very serious crimes today.

## 2. Immigration Rules Could Chill Philadelphia’s Economy 

Philadelphia’s economy depends partly on international students. New federal rules about student visas are causing concern. These rules might make it harder for students from other countries to study in the US. Experts say this change could have a **huge chilling effect** on the city’s schools and businesses. International students spend money and bring new ideas, which help the local economy grow. If fewer students come, many universities and companies might lose money. The future of Philadelphia’s economy could change a lot because of these immigration rules.

## 3. US Job Market Remains Stable Despite Slow Hiring 

In September, US companies hired fewer workers than expected. Only 29,000 new jobs were added, which is much lower than usual. Even with this slow growth, experts say the labor market is still **on a steady track**. This means the overall job situation is stable and not getting worse. Many people are still working, and unemployment remains low. However, the slow hiring may show that companies are cautious because of economic uncertainty. What happens next will depend on how the economy grows in the coming months.

## 4. Silicon Valley Faces Challenges Hiring Top Tech Talent 

The US Department of Homeland Security proposed a big increase in fees for H-1B visas. These visas allow tech companies to hire skilled workers from other countries. The new fee would be $103,265, much higher than before. Many Silicon Valley companies worry about this because it will be harder and more expensive to **hire top talent**. Hiring skilled workers is important for innovation and growth in the tech industry. Some companies are also trying new ideas, like creating special tech academies that train students in two years. The future of tech hiring may change a lot because of these rules.
