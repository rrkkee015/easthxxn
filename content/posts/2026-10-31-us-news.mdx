## 1. Court Blocks Trump’s Latest Attempt to Control California Ballots  
  

The court has refused former President Trump’s recent effort to take control of ballots in California. Trump wanted to **impound** these ballots to review them closely. However, the court said this was not allowed and rejected the request. This fight is part of ongoing disputes about election results and voting rights in the US. The decision shows the court’s role in protecting fair election processes. It also means the ballots will stay secure and be counted as planned. This case may continue, but for now, California’s election results are safe from this action. The public is watching closely because election fairness is very important for democracy.

## 2. Five Big Ways Trump Changed US Immigration  
  

A recent report looks at five important ways former President Trump changed immigration laws in the US. His policies made it harder for many people to enter or stay in the country legally. For example, he reduced the number of refugees allowed and increased border controls. These changes caused strong opinions and protests from different groups. Some say the policies made the system stricter and safer. Others believe they hurt families and immigrants seeking a better life. These immigration rules still affect many people today and could influence future laws. Understanding these changes helps us see how government decisions impact immigration.

## 3. Construction Jobs Grow Mainly in Half of US Cities  
  

Construction job growth in the US is happening mostly in about half of the country’s metro areas. This means some cities have many new construction jobs while others see little change. The construction field is important because it shows how much building and development is happening. More jobs in this area can also mean a stronger local economy. However, people living in cities without growth may worry about fewer opportunities. Experts watch these patterns to understand where the economy is improving or slowing down. The trend may affect decisions about where workers move for jobs or where companies invest money.

## 4. FBI Arrest Raises Spy Concerns in US Tech Companies  
  

The FBI recently arrested a person connected to spying inside a US technology company. This case has raised fears that foreign spies might be trying to steal secrets from American tech firms. Many tech companies work with sensitive information, so security is very important. The arrest shows how serious the government is about protecting technology and data. Experts warn businesses to watch out for suspicious activities. This situation could make companies increase their security measures. The tech industry plays a big role in the US economy, so keeping it safe is a top priority.
