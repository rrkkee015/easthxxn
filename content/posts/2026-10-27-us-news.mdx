## 1. U.S. Manufacturing Jobs Are Growing, But Hiring Has Changed  
  

The U.S. manufacturing industry is **adding jobs again** after some slow years. Many factories are hiring more workers because demand for goods is increasing. But the way companies hire has **changed** a lot. They now look for workers with special skills and often use new technology to help with work. Some jobs that used to be simple now need more training. This is important because manufacturing is a big part of the economy. If companies keep hiring, it could mean better pay and more opportunities for workers. However, workers may need to learn new skills to keep up with the changes.

## 2. International Students in the U.S. Face Uncertain Futures  
  

Many international students come to the U.S. to study every year. But now, **amid** stricter visa and immigration rules, some students are worried about their future. The current government has made it harder to get or keep student visas. This has caused fear and confusion among students who want to stay in the U.S. after graduation. Schools and communities are trying to support these students, but the situation is still difficult. This issue matters because many students bring important skills and knowledge to the U.S. economy. What happens next will affect both students and American universities.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **amid** — ~중에, ~가 있는 상황에서  
>   _Many students are nervous **amid** the new visa rules._  
> - **stricter** — 더 엄격한  
>   _The government made **stricter** immigration policies._  
> - **uncertain futures** — 불확실한 미래  
>   _International students worry about their **uncertain futures**._

## 3. FBI Arrest Raises Concerns About Spies in U.S. Tech Companies  
  

Recently, the FBI arrested a person suspected of spying inside a U.S. technology company. This case has **revived fears** that foreign spies might work for or steal secrets from American tech firms. Technology companies are very important for the economy and national security. If spies get inside these companies, they could cause serious damage. This news has made both companies and the government think more about security. They may add new rules to protect important information. People worry about privacy and trust in technology as this story develops.

## 4. Israeli Election Puts Netanyahu’s Political Future at Risk  
  

Israel recently held an election that could decide the future of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. His **political survival** is on the line because the vote is very close. Netanyahu has been a key leader in Israel for many years. If he loses support, he might have to leave office. This election is important not only for Israel but also for U.S. foreign policy in the Middle East. The results may affect peace talks and regional stability. People around the world are watching to see what happens next.

> - **on the line** — 위험에 처한, 걸려 있는

> - **lose support** — 지지를 잃다
