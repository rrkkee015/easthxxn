## 4. Proposed H-1B Visa Fee Increase Worries Silicon Valley 

The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has proposed increasing the H-1B visa fee to $103,265. This is a large rise that worries many companies in Silicon Valley. The H-1B visa lets US companies hire talented workers from other countries. Tech companies say this fee increase will make it harder to **hire top talent**. They fear it could slow down innovation and growth in the industry. The DHS says the fees help cover costs for managing the visa program. Now, companies and lawmakers will discuss if this increase is fair or too high. The decision will affect many skilled workers and the tech economy.
