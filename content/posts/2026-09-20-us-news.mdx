## 1. US and Denmark Agree on Greenland’s Security 

The United States and Denmark have reached an important deal about Greenland’s security. This agreement comes after President Trump suggested buying Greenland, which caused tension between the countries. Greenland is a large island with strategic importance in the Arctic region. Both countries want to work together to keep the area safe from other countries’ influence. This deal shows how the US and Denmark are trying to **strengthen ties** and avoid conflict. It is important because Greenland has valuable natural resources and a key location for military defense. Next, the countries will likely share more information and plan joint actions to protect Greenland’s future.

## 2. US Job Growth Surprises with 162,000 New Jobs in August 

The US economy added 162,000 jobs in August, which is more than double what experts expected. This strong job growth shows that many companies are hiring again, even though some parts of the economy face challenges. However, wages are not growing fast enough to keep up with inflation, which means people may still struggle to afford things. The increase in jobs puts more pressure on the government to handle inflation carefully. Experts will watch future reports to see if this trend continues. Overall, the job market looks **stronger than expected**, but challenges remain for workers.

> - **job growth** — 일자리 증가

## 3. New US Immigration Policies Reflect 1920s Attitudes 

The current US immigration policy reminds many experts of the 1920s, a time when the country limited certain groups of newcomers. The government is trying to control who can enter and stay in the country, especially focusing on certain nationalities or backgrounds. This change affects many migrant families and children, with some facing faster removal from the US. Critics say this approach is too strict and unfair. Supporters argue it is necessary for national security. This policy shift is important because immigration has always shaped America’s culture and economy. It will be important to watch how these changes affect communities in the future.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **removal of migrant children** — 이주 아동의 추방

## 4. America’s Tech Hubs Are Moving Beyond Silicon Valley 

Silicon Valley is no longer the only important tech hub in the US. Many other cities are growing fast as centers for technology and innovation. Places like Austin, Miami, and Denver attract tech companies and workers because of lower costs and good quality of life. This change means the tech scene in America is becoming more **diverse and spread out**. It also creates new job opportunities in different regions. However, some conservatives have started to disagree with Silicon Valley on certain issues, showing the tech world is also becoming more divided. The shift might change where future tech investments and jobs will be.
