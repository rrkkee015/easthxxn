## 1. Concerns Grow as ICE Targets International Students at UCLA  
  

International students at UCLA are facing new challenges because ICE, the U.S. immigration agency, has increased its focus on them. ICE is checking if students follow their visa rules strictly. If students do not meet the rules, they could lose their status and must leave the country. UCLA and other schools are trying to support these students during this stressful time. This situation is important because many universities rely on international students for diversity and funding. If these students feel unsafe, they might choose to study in other countries. The future depends on how schools and the government handle the issue to protect students’ rights and education opportunities.

A flight to Israel faced serious danger, but a pilot, a plumber, and a dentist helped save the plane. The pilot showed great skill and calm under pressure. The plumber and dentist also helped passengers stay safe during the emergency. Many people called them “heroes” because they worked together to avoid a disaster. This story reminds us how ordinary people can do extraordinary things in difficult moments. It also shows the importance of teamwork and quick thinking during emergencies. This event will be remembered as an example of courage.

## 3. U.S. Job Openings Fall in August but Layoffs Are Low  
  

In August, the number of open jobs in the U.S. went down to 7.1 million. This means there are fewer job vacancies than before. However, layoffs, or job losses, stayed low. This is a good sign because it shows companies are not firing many workers. The job market is still strong but may be slowing down a bit. Experts say this could mean the economy is changing and employers are being more careful. The White House said the labor market is on an **upward trajectory**, which means it should get better soon. We will watch the next months to see if this trend continues.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job openings** — 일자리 공석  
>   _There are many **job openings** in the tech industry._  
> - **layoffs remain low** — 해고가 적다  
>   _Despite the recession, **layoffs remain low**._  
> - **job market** — 취업 시장  
>   _The **job market** can be competitive._  
> - **upward trajectory** — 상승 추세  
>   _The economy is on an **upward trajectory** according to experts._

## 4. Trump and AI CEOs Sign Safety Pact, Support Data Center Growth  
  

Former President Trump and leaders of major AI companies signed a voluntary safety pact. This agreement means they promise to work together to keep AI technology safe for people. They also support expanding data centers, which are places where big computers store and process data. This pact is important because AI is growing fast and can be risky if not controlled well. By signing, these leaders want to make sure AI helps society without causing harm. The pact is voluntary, so it depends on companies keeping their promises. The future of AI safety will need careful attention from both government and tech businesses.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **sign a pact** — 협약서에 서명하다  
>   _Countries **signed a pact** to reduce pollution._  
> - **work together** — 함께 일하다  
>   _The companies will **work together** to improve safety._  
> - **keep promises** — 약속을 지키다  
>   _It is important to **keep promises** in agreements._  
> - **grow fast** — 빠르게 성장하다  
>   _AI technology is **growing fast** worldwide._
