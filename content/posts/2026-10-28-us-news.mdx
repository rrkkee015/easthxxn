## 1. Important Changes in Immigrant Health Policies After Midterm Elections  


The recent midterm elections in the US have brought attention to **immigrant health policy** changes. Many immigrant communities face challenges getting medical care, and new laws may improve their access. Some states want to offer more health services to immigrants, while others are more strict. These policies affect millions of people and will change how hospitals and clinics serve them. It is important because good health care helps immigrants live better lives and contribute to their communities. Experts say this is a step toward a fairer health system. We will watch how these new policies work in the coming months.

## 2. US Manufacturing Jobs Are Growing but Hiring Is Changing  


The US manufacturing sector is hiring more workers again after some slow months. Factories are adding jobs, but the way companies hire people is not the same as before. New rules and technology change what skills workers need. Some older jobs are disappearing, and new ones require more training and computer skills. This matters because manufacturing is an important part of the economy and job market. Workers may need to learn new skills to keep up with these changes. Experts say this shift will continue as technology improves.

> - **training** — 훈련, 교육

## 3. US Tech Companies Are Building a Military Role in AI Development  


Big technology companies in the US are working closely with the military to develop artificial intelligence (AI). This partnership is creating a new type of **military–industrial complex**. AI can help with defense, security, and new weapons, but it also raises questions about ethics and power. These companies invest a lot in AI research and get contracts from the government. This work can change how wars are fought in the future. People are watching carefully to see how this affects privacy and safety. The rise of AI in the military is a big step in technology and national security.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **military–industrial complex** — 군산복합체 (군사와 산업의 밀접한 관계)  
>   _The **military–industrial complex** influences government spending._  
> - **artificial intelligence (AI)** — 인공지능  
>   _Many companies invest in **artificial intelligence (AI)** research._  
> - **ethics** — 윤리  
>   _Questions about **ethics** arise when AI is used in weapons._  
> - **partnership** — 협력 관계  
>   _The **partnership** between tech firms and the military is growing._

## 4. Korean High School Students Gain Experience in US High-Tech Industry  


A group of high school students from Siheung, Korea, recently visited the US to learn about the high-tech industry. They worked with AI and robotics experts and saw how advanced technology is used in real life. This experience helps students understand the global tech market and prepares them for future jobs. The program shows how education and industry can work together to build skills. It is important because technology is changing fast, and young people need to keep up. Students said this visit inspired them to study harder and dream bigger.
