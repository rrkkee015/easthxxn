## 2. State Immigration Policies Are Shifting From Help to Limits  


In 2026, many US states are changing how they handle immigration. The new trend shows that states are moving from offering **protections** for immigrants to putting more **restrictions** or limits on them. This means some states have made laws that make it harder for immigrants to get certain rights or services. These changes can affect many families and workers who live in the US but are from other countries. The reason for this shift is often political, as some leaders want stricter immigration rules. Experts say this can make life more difficult for immigrants and may change the US economy. We will watch how these state policies develop and how the federal government reacts.

## 3. US Job Market Shows Strong Growth in August  


The US job market is showing good signs with a big increase in jobs in August. About 162,000 new jobs were created last month. This hiring **burst** surprised some experts because it shows companies are still growing. When more people have jobs, they have more money to spend, which helps the economy. However, this growth makes some worry about **inflation**. Inflation means prices for things go up, so money does not buy as much as before. The government and the Federal Reserve will watch these numbers closely to decide if they need to change interest rates or other policies. Many hope the job market stays strong without causing too much inflation.
