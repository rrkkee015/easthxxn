## 1. Trump Promises $5,000 ‘Dividend’ if Republicans Win Midterms  
  

Donald Trump has promised a **dividend** of $5,000 to U.S. voters if the Republican Party wins the upcoming midterm elections. This idea means that people would get money as a kind of reward or payment. Trump says this will help families and workers who have been struggling with the economy. Many experts think this promise is an attempt to win more votes by appealing to voters' financial concerns. However, some people question if this plan is realistic or how it would be paid for. The promise could change the way political campaigns work in the future. The midterms will be an important test to see if this idea has real support.

## 2. U.S. Immigration Policy: Still Maddeningly Inconsistent  
  

U.S. immigration policy often feels confusing and **inconsistent**. Some rules change quickly, while others stay the same for years. For example, the government sometimes says “Help Wanted” because they need workers, but at the same time, it tells some immigrants to “Leave Now.” This creates frustration for immigrants and employers. The policies also change depending on politics, which makes it hard to understand what will happen next. Many people want clearer, more fair immigration laws. How the U.S. solves these problems will affect millions of lives.

## 3. Strong Job Growth Shows U.S. Economy Is Healthy  
  

The U.S. economy shows strength with a **burst** of 162,000 new jobs added in August. This number is higher than many expected and shows companies are hiring more workers. A strong job market can help families earn more money and feel secure. However, it can also cause worries about inflation, which means prices for things go up. The government will watch the job numbers closely to decide what to do next about the economy. Experts say this kind of job growth is a **bright spot** in a world with many economic problems.

## 4. How a Blacklisted Chinese Tech Giant Bought America’s Best AI Chips  
  

A Chinese technology company that is on a U.S. **blacklist** still found ways to buy America’s best artificial intelligence (AI) chips. These chips are very important for creating smart machines and software. The U.S. government put this company on the blacklist because of security concerns and trade rules. But the company used other businesses and methods to get the chips anyway. This situation shows how technology and politics are connected. Experts warn this could create problems for U.S. tech leadership in the future.
