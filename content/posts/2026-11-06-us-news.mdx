## 1. Democrats Make Big Gains in 2026 Midterm Elections

The Democratic Party **flipped** over 40 seats, which means they won many seats that were held by the Republican Party. This is important because it changes the balance of power in Congress. The Republican Party is trying to **grapple with** these surprising losses, meaning they are struggling to understand and handle the situation. Midterm elections happen halfway through a president’s term and can affect how laws are made. This election result could impact future policies and political decisions in the U.S. People are watching closely to see what this means for the next steps in government.
