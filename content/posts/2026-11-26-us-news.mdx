## 1. Macy’s Thanksgiving Parade Postponed Due to Severe Weather  
  

This year, Macy’s famous Thanksgiving Day parade will not happen as planned. The parade was **postponed** because of severe weather, including strong winds and rain. Safety is the main concern since balloons and performers could be at risk in bad weather. Macy’s said they want to keep everyone safe and will announce a new date soon. This parade is a long-time holiday tradition in the US, so many people were disappointed. Weather can **disrupt** important events like this. The parade organizers hope to **reschedule** it when conditions are better. Watching this event live or on TV is a favorite part of many families’ Thanksgiving celebrations.

## 2. New Immigration Policies Could Change US State Rules  
  

The US government is looking at new immigration policies that will affect states differently. Some states want to offer more **protections** to immigrants, while others plan to make stricter **restrictions**. These changes are important because they affect people’s lives, such as where they can live and work. The 287(g) program lets local officers help with immigration enforcement, but it is controversial. The upcoming elections may also change immigration laws depending on who wins. This topic is important because immigration affects the economy and communities. People will watch closely to see how these policies develop.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **protections** — 보호 조치  
>   _Some cities offer **protections** for immigrants to keep families together._  
> - **restrictions** — 제한, 규제  
>   _New travel **restrictions** were announced to control the spread of disease._  
> - **enforcement** — 집행, 이행  
>   _The police focus on the **enforcement** of traffic laws._

## 3. US Job Market: Over One Million Jobs Still Missing  
  

The US job market shows mixed signals. More than one million jobs are still **missing** compared to before the pandemic. Some companies, like Kroger, are cutting thousands of corporate jobs in the US but hiring in other countries. At the same time, the US economy keeps creating new jobs overall. However, inflation is making it hard for many Americans to **keep up** with rising costs. This means even though there are jobs, people may not feel better financially. The job market is changing fast, and workers need to watch the trends closely.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **missing** — 부족한, 없는  
>   _There are still many **missing** jobs in the industry after the crisis._  
> - **keep up** — 따라가다, 유지하다  
>   _It’s hard to **keep up** with the rising prices of food and housing._  
> - **cutting jobs** — 일자리 감축하다  
>   _The company is **cutting jobs** to reduce costs._

## 4. Korean Femtech Startups Pitch to Silicon Valley Investors  
  

A group of Korean femtech startups recently made their first big pitch to Silicon Valley investors. Femtech means technology designed to help women’s health and wellness. The startups showed their new ideas at the Dreamkium 2026 Dream Challenge. This is important because Silicon Valley is a major place for tech funding and growth. These companies hope to get money and support to grow their businesses in the US market. This event also shows how international startups are becoming more active in the US tech scene. It could lead to more innovation for women’s health everywhere.
