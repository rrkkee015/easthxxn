## 1. Alaska Fights Against Trump Administration’s Senate Ballot Demand 

Alaska officials are asking a judge to **deny** the Trump administration’s request to throw out some Senate ballots. The government said it was an **impossible** task to count those ballots fairly. Alaska says the demand is unfair and could harm voters’ rights. The ballots are important because they affect who will represent Alaska in the Senate. This case shows how election rules can become part of political fights. If the judge agrees with Alaska, the ballots will count in the election. This story is important because it shows how the legal system helps protect voting rights.

## 3. The Strangest Job Market in the US Turns Three Years Old 

The US job market is now three years into a strange pattern. People are not hiring many new workers, but at the same time, companies are not firing many employees. This low-hire, low-fire economy is unusual because usually jobs change more often. Experts say this situation shows how both workers and companies feel uncertain about the future. Some industries are growing fast, but others are slow. This trend affects how people find jobs and how businesses plan for growth. Understanding this market helps workers and employers prepare for changes ahead.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job market** — 취업 시장  
>   _The **job market** is competitive for new graduates._  
> - **low-hire, low-fire** — 적게 고용하고 적게 해고하는  
>   _The company’s **low-hire, low-fire** policy keeps its workforce stable._  
> - **uncertain** — 불확실한, 확신이 없는  
>   _Many people feel **uncertain** about their jobs during economic changes._  
> - **industry** — 산업, 업계  
>   _The tech **industry** is growing faster than many others._
