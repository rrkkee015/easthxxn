Voting can **bring about** changes that improve their future and protect their rights. Groups like HIAS encourage people to vote because every vote can **make a difference**. If more immigrants and refugees vote, their voices will be stronger in government decisions. This could lead to better opportunities and fairer treatment for them across the country.

## 3. U.S. Job Openings Fall Slightly but Labor Market Stays Strong 

In August, job openings in the United States dropped to 7.1 million, showing a small slowdown in hiring. However, experts say the labor market is still **sturdy**, meaning many people can find jobs and the economy is doing okay. This change might mean companies are being more careful about hiring new workers. Some signs suggest a slower economy, but job opportunities remain available. The government and businesses will watch these numbers closely to plan for the future. Workers might feel a bit less pressure, but it is still important to stay prepared for changes in the job market.

## 4. FBI Arrest Raises Concerns About Spies in U.S. Tech Companies 

The FBI recently arrested a person suspected of spying inside a US technology company. This case has raised fears that foreign spies may try to steal secrets from important tech firms. Many companies work on advanced technology that is valuable and sensitive. The government is now paying more attention to security inside these businesses. Experts say this could affect hiring and how companies protect their information. It is important for tech companies to **police themselves** to keep data safe and avoid spying problems in the future.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **spy** — 스파이, 첩자  
>   _The company is worried about **spies** stealing their technology._  
> - **pay attention to** — 주의를 기울이다  
>   _We need to **pay attention to** security risks._  
> - **police themselves** — 스스로 감시하다, 스스로 관리하다  
>   _Tech companies must **police themselves** to prevent leaks._
