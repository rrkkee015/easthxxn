## 1. Five Ways Trump Changed US Immigration Policies 

Former President Trump made many changes to immigration rules in the United States. These changes affected how people enter and stay in the country. For example, he increased border security and limited the number of refugees allowed. He also changed rules about work visas and deportations. These policies have had a big impact on immigrants and their families. Some people support these changes to protect jobs and safety, while others think they are too strict. Understanding these changes is important because immigration affects many parts of American life. The debate about immigration laws will likely continue in the future.

## 4. Big Tech Builds Military-Industrial Complex with AI Technology 

Big technology companies are working closely with the military to develop new AI tools. This partnership is sometimes called a military–industrial complex. AI can help with defense by improving drones, security systems, and data analysis. However, some people worry about the risks of using AI in war. This cooperation shows how technology is changing the way countries prepare for future conflicts. It also raises questions about ethics and control of advanced technology. Watching how tech companies and the military work together is important for understanding the future of security.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **military-industrial complex** — 군산 복합체  
>   _The **military-industrial complex** links the defense industry with the government._  
> - **AI technology** — 인공지능 기술  
>   _**AI technology** is used in many new military applications._  
> - **partnership** — 협력 관계  
>   _The **partnership** between tech firms and the military is growing._
