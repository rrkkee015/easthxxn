## 1. Alaska Election Brings Democrats Strong Senate Majority  
  

In a recent election, Mary Peltola won against Republican Dan Sullivan in Alaska. This victory gives the Democrats 56 seats in the US Senate. The Senate is very important because it helps make laws for the country. Democrats now have a **strong majority**, which means they can pass laws more easily. This result shows a shift in Alaska's politics, which has often supported Republicans. Experts think this change could affect important decisions about health care, climate, and the economy. The election also reflects how voters want new ideas and leadership. We will watch how this new Senate works in the next months.

## 2. Calls for Immigration Policy Changes After Child's Detention  
  

In San Diego, officials are asking for changes in immigration policies after an 8-year-old child was detained. Detention means holding someone in a place, often for legal reasons. Many people feel that it is not right to hold young children in detention centers. This case has brought attention to how the US treats immigrant families. Some states want stronger protections for immigrants, while others support more restrictions. The debate is about finding a balance between safety and kindness. Changing immigration laws could affect many families who come to the US for a better life. We will see if lawmakers listen to these calls soon.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **immigration policies** — 이민 정책  \

> - **detained** — 구금된  \

## 4. Silicon Valley Supports Nvidia’s Jensen Huang After Departure  
  

Jensen Huang, the former CEO of Nvidia, recently left his position. Nvidia is a major company in technology, especially in artificial intelligence (AI). Many people in Silicon Valley, the center of tech innovation, are still supporting Huang. They believe his ideas helped make Nvidia a leader in AI chips. Even after leaving, Huang’s influence remains strong. This shows how one person’s vision can shape the future of technology. Experts think this support will help new AI projects grow in the US.
