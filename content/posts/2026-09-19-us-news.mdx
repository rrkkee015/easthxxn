## 1. Trump Bans CNN and Other Media from White House  


Former President Donald Trump has banned CNN, MSNBC, and Politico reporters from the White House press briefings. He said these outlets spread **fake news** and unfair stories about him. This decision has raised concerns about **freedom of the press** and how the government controls information. Many people believe the press should be able to report freely, even if the news is critical. The White House says it wants to protect its message and avoid **biased coverage**. This ban could affect how Americans get news from official sources. It is still unclear if this ban will continue or change under new leadership.

## 2. US and Denmark Agree on Greenland Security Deal  


The United States and Denmark have made a new security agreement about Greenland. Greenland is a large island owned by Denmark but is very close to the US. President Trump said this deal will help protect the Arctic region from other countries like Russia and China. This is important because Greenland has many natural resources and a strategic location for military use. The agreement means the US can have more access and cooperation with Denmark in Greenland. It also shows stronger ties between the two countries for future security. This deal might change the balance of power in the Arctic area.

## 3. US Job Market Shows Strong Growth  


The US economy added 162,000 jobs in August, which is much more than experts expected. This is a good sign because it means companies are hiring more workers and the economy is getting stronger. More jobs can help people earn money and spend on goods and services. However, this strong job growth could also make inflation worse. Inflation means prices go up and money buys less than before. The government may need to watch this carefully and decide if it should change interest rates. Overall, the job market is an important factor for how the economy will perform in the future.

> - **job market** — 취업 시장  
>   _The **job market** is improving after the pandemic._  
> - **strong growth** — 강한 성장  
>   _The company showed **strong growth** in sales last quarter._  
> - **inflation** — 인플레이션, 물가 상승

## 4. America’s Tech Scene Moves Beyond Silicon Valley  


America’s technology industry is growing in many cities, not just Silicon Valley. Cities like Austin, Miami, and Denver are becoming new tech hubs. This is because companies want to find new talent and avoid high costs in California. The change means more jobs and opportunities in different parts of the country. It also helps smaller cities develop their economies with new businesses. This shift could change where the most important tech companies and workers are located in the future. People say this makes the tech industry stronger and more diverse.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **tech hub** — 기술 중심지  
>   _Austin is becoming a major **tech hub** in the US._  
> - **new talent** — 새로운 인재  
>   _Companies move to find **new talent** for their teams._  
> - **high costs** — 높은 비용  
>   _Many companies leave Silicon Valley because of **high costs**._
