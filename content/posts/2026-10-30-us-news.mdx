## 1. Trump Changes Rally Plans Ahead of Midterms  
  

Former President Donald Trump has changed his plans for upcoming political rallies. He canceled a rally in a state that usually supports him strongly. Experts say he is **shifting strategy** to focus on different areas before the midterm elections. Midterms are very important because they decide which political party controls Congress. Trump’s new approach might help him win more votes where the race is close. However, some supporters are surprised by this change. The next few weeks will show if his plan works or not.

## 2. Five Major Ways Trump Changed US Immigration  
  

Trump made big changes to immigration rules during his time as president. These changes affected how people can enter and stay in the United States. Some of his policies made it harder for people to get visas or asylum. Others focused on building stronger border security. These changes still affect immigration today, even after Trump left office. Experts say these policies have caused mixed feelings among politicians and immigrants. Understanding these changes helps us see how immigration laws can change quickly.

## 3. Construction Job Growth Focused in Half of US Cities  
  

New reports show that construction jobs are growing, but mostly in half of the big US cities. This means many places see more building and hiring, while others do not. Construction work is important because it helps build homes, offices, and roads. Experts say this growth could help local economies where jobs increase. But people in cities without growth might face harder job searches. The government and companies might need to find ways to spread job opportunities more evenly.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job growth** — 일자리 증가  
>   _The city experienced strong **job growth** last year._  
> - **local economies** — 지역 경제  
>   _New factories can boost **local economies** by creating jobs._  
> - **job searches** — 일자리 찾기  
>   _Many people spend weeks on **job searches** before finding work._

## 4. Anthropic's IPO Could Impact Tech and Finance  
  

Anthropic, an artificial intelligence company, is planning to sell shares to the public for the first time. This is called an IPO, or initial public offering. Experts say this event could have a **trickle-down effect** on both Wall Street and Silicon Valley. It means the success or failure of Anthropic might affect many other companies and investors. The AI industry is growing fast, and this IPO shows how important AI technology has become. People are watching closely to see how this will change business and technology in the future.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **IPO (initial public offering)** — 기업공개  
>   _The startup prepared for its **IPO** to raise money from investors._  
> - **trickle-down effect** — 파급 효과  
>   _The new policy had a **trickle-down effect** on small businesses._  
> - **investors** — 투자자들  
>   _Many **investors** are interested in technology companies._
