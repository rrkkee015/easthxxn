## 1. U.S. Lawmakers See Growing Risks in AI but No Easy Solutions 

The U.S. lawmakers agree that artificial intelligence (AI) brings new **risks** that are rising fast. They think AI can change many parts of life, like jobs and privacy. But they also say there is no **quick fix** to control these risks right now. Lawmakers want to study AI more before making strong rules. Some worry AI could be used in harmful ways without rules. This matters because AI is growing fast and affects millions of people. In the future, we might see new laws about AI safety and ethics. For now, the government is trying to understand how to balance AI’s benefits with its dangers.

## 2. Immigration Authority Rules Updated by ICE 

The U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) shared updates about Section 287(g) of the Immigration and Nationality Act. This law lets local law enforcement work with federal immigration officers. The goal is to find and remove people who do not follow immigration rules. The update explains how this authority is given or **delegated** to local agencies. This is important because it affects how immigration laws are enforced in many communities. Some people support the cooperation for safety reasons, but others worry about fairness and rights. The government will continue to review and adjust these rules over time.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **delegated** — 위임된  
>   _Authority was **delegated** to the local police._  
> - **enforce** — 집행하다  
>   _The police **enforce** the laws to keep order._  
> - **cooperation** — 협력  
>   _Good **cooperation** between agencies helps solve problems._  
> - **fairness** — 공정성  
>   _Everyone expects **fairness** in how laws are applied._

## 3. U.S. Job Market Shows Strength with New Hiring Numbers 

In August, the U.S. added 162,000 new jobs, showing the job market is still **strong**. This hiring **burst** means more people found work even with higher prices and inflation. The new jobs come in many industries, helping the economy grow. Experts say this puts more attention on inflation and how the Federal Reserve will act. A strong job market usually means more people have money to spend. However, some worry that inflation could still cause problems. The government will watch these numbers closely in the coming months.

## 4. America’s Top Tech Hubs Move Beyond Silicon Valley 

The U.S. tech industry is changing. Silicon Valley is no longer the top place for tech jobs and talent. New cities like Austin, Miami, and Denver are growing fast as tech hubs. This shift shows that tech companies and workers want more options outside California. The change may bring more jobs to different parts of the country. It also means more competition between cities to attract tech business. This trend could shape where new technology grows in the future.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **tech hub** — 기술 중심지  
>   _Seattle is a growing **tech hub** in the northwest._  
> - **shift** — 변화, 이동  
>   _There is a big **shift** in where people work after the pandemic._  
> - **talent** — 인재  
>   _Companies compete to hire the best **talent**._  
> - **competition** — 경쟁  
>   _Cities face **competition** to attract new businesses._
