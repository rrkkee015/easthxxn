## 1. Trump Warns Tech Firms About Building Data Centers Abroad 

The former president, Donald Trump, has threatened **tremendous penalties** for American tech companies that build data centers in other countries. He says this could hurt US security and jobs. Data centers are important because they store and manage a lot of information online. Trump wants these centers to stay in the US to protect information and keep jobs at home. Many tech companies are watching closely because moving data centers abroad can sometimes save money. The issue shows how technology and politics are connected. It might cause companies to rethink where they put their data centers in the future.

## 2. Trump Administration’s Immigration Policy Changes Face Pushback 

The Trump administration tried to remove important legal protections for many immigrants. This process is called "mass delegalization." It means many people who had some legal rights might lose them. However, some courts and organizations have stopped these changes from happening quickly. One example is the "Duration of Status" policy, which lets certain immigrants stay in the US as long as they follow visa rules. The government wanted to end this policy, but a court blocked this effort. Immigration remains a strong and difficult issue in the US, affecting many families and workers. What happens next could change life for many immigrants.

> ### 영어 표현 학습
> - **legal protections** — 법적 보호  
>   _The new law offers **legal protections** to workers against unfair treatment._  
> - **mass delegalization** — 대규모 불법화  
>   _The policy led to **mass delegalization** of many immigrants' status._  
> - **pushback** — 반발, 저항  
>   _There was strong **pushback** against the proposed changes._  
> - **blocked** — 막히다, 저지되다  
>   _The court **blocked** the government’s plan to end the policy._

## 3. US Job Market Shows Signs of Revival With More Job Postings 

The US labor market is showing signs of improvement. Recent reports say that job postings have increased, meaning more employers are looking for workers. In August, companies added about 162,000 new jobs, which is a big number after some slow months. This hiring increase helps the economy and may affect inflation, which is the rise in prices for goods and services. When more people work, they have more money to spend. But if hiring grows too fast, prices might go up even more. Experts will watch these numbers carefully to understand the next steps for the economy.

> ### 영어 표현 학습
> - **job market** — 노동시장, 일자리 시장  
>   _The **job market** is competitive for new college graduates._  
> - **signs of revival** — 회복의 조짐  
>   _There are **signs of revival** in the economy after the slowdown._  
> - **job postings** — 구인 공고  
>   _The number of **job postings** increased last month._  
> - **inflation** — 물가 상승, 인플레이션

## 4. The “Silicon Valley Right” Is Changing the Tech Industry’s Politics 

In recent years, a new political group called the “Silicon Valley Right” has grown in the technology industry. This group supports more conservative ideas, which is different from the usual liberal views in tech. Their rise is changing how tech companies think about politics and business. Some people say this shift will affect big decisions about technology, privacy, and US-China relations. The political divide in Silicon Valley also shows how technology is linked to bigger global issues. How this group influences the tech world will be interesting to watch in the coming years.

> ### 영어 표현 학습
> - **Silicon Valley Right** — 실리콘밸리 보수파  
>   _The **Silicon Valley Right** supports more conservative tech policies._  
> - **political divide** — 정치적 분열  
>   _There is a clear **political divide** in many companies today._  
> - **big decisions** — 중요한 결정  
>   _The CEO must make **big decisions** about the company’s future._  
> - **linked to** — ~와 연결된, 관련된  
>   _Technology is closely **linked to** global politics._
