## 1. Hurricane Isaias Hits Gulf Coast with Strong Winds and Rain  


Hurricane Isaias made landfall on the Gulf Coast, bringing heavy **wind** and rain. The storm caused power outages and flooding in many areas. Officials warned people to stay indoors and be ready for possible emergencies. This hurricane is part of a busy storm season affecting the US. It damaged homes and blocked roads, creating challenges for emergency workers. People are now working to clean up and restore services. The government and local teams continue to monitor the situation. Staying safe and following official advice is very important during storms like this.

## 2. Farmers Say Immigration Policies Are Raising Food Prices  


Farmers in the US are saying that new immigration policies are making food more expensive. Many farms rely on immigrant workers to pick crops and do other hard jobs. When fewer workers come, farmers cannot harvest all their crops on time. This shortage can lead to less food and higher prices in stores. Farmers are asking the government to improve immigration rules so they can find enough workers. This issue affects not just the farmers but also consumers who buy food. It shows how immigration policies can impact the whole economy. The government is still working on solutions.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **immigration policy** — 이민 정책  
>   _The **immigration policy** affects how many workers can enter the country._  
> - **raise prices** — 가격을 올리다  
>   _New rules may **raise prices** on fresh fruits._  
> - **harvest crops** — 작물을 수확하다  
>   _Farmers need workers to **harvest crops** quickly._  
> - **shortage** — 부족  
>   _There is a **shortage** of labor on many farms._

## 3. US Labor Market Shows Slow but Steady Job Growth in September  


The US added 29,000 new jobs in September, which is slower than before but still shows a **steady** job market. Many experts say this is a normal change after months of strong hiring. The number of job openings also dropped a bit but remains high at 7.1 million. This means many companies still want to hire workers. The slow growth may be because of economic uncertainties or changes in business needs. Workers may find it a little harder to get new jobs, but the market is not falling. Overall, the labor market looks stable for now.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **steady** — 안정된  
>   _The labor market is **steady**, with slow but positive growth._  
> - **job opening** — 구인 공석  
>   _There are many **job openings** in the technology sector._  
> - **economic uncertainty** — 경제적 불확실성  
>   _**Economic uncertainty** makes companies cautious about hiring._  
> - **slow growth** — 느린 성장  
>   _The company reported **slow growth** in sales this quarter._

## 4. DHS Plans Big Increase in H-1B Visa Fees, Causing Concern in Tech Industry  


The US Department of Homeland Security (DHS) proposed raising the fee for H-1B visas to over $100,000. The H-1B visa allows many skilled workers, especially in tech, to work legally in the US. Tech companies say this big increase will make it harder to **hire top talent** from other countries. They worry it could slow down innovation and growth in Silicon Valley. The government says the higher fee will support enforcement and reduce fraud. But companies and experts ask how this change will affect the US economy and job market. The proposal is still under review, and more discussions will follow.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **hire top talent** — 최고의 인재를 고용하다  
>   _Tech firms want to **hire top talent** from around the world._  
> - **propose** — 제안하다  
>   _The government **proposed** new rules for the visa program._  
> - **slow down** — 느리게 하다, 지연시키다  
>   _Higher fees may **slow down** hiring in the tech industry._  
> - **under review** — 검토 중인  
>   _The new policy is still **under review** by officials._
