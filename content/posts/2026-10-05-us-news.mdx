## 1. US Withdraws B-1 Bombers from UK Base Over Iran Concerns  
  

The US has decided to **withdraw** its B-1 bomber aircraft from a military base in the UK. This move comes because of growing fears about possible threats from Iran. The bombers were stationed at Fairford base to show strength and support to allies. But now, the US says it will move them to a safer location. This change shows how important it is for the US to keep its military forces ready but safe. It also reflects worries about rising tensions in the Middle East. Experts say this could affect how the US and UK work together on security in the future. We will watch closely to see what happens next.

## 2. Supreme Court to Decide on Trump’s Immigration Detention Policy  
  

The US Supreme Court is preparing to make an important decision about a policy from the Trump administration. This policy requires mandatory detention of certain immigrants while they wait for legal hearings. Supporters say it helps keep the country's borders safe. Opponents argue that it can be unfair and might violate immigrants' rights. The case will affect how immigration laws are enforced in the future. Many people are watching closely because the ruling could change how immigration detention works nationwide. The decision may also impact ongoing debates about immigration reform in the US.

## 3. US Job Market Remains Stable Despite Slower Hiring in September  
  

In September, the US job market showed weaker than expected hiring numbers. But experts say the labor market is still on a **steady track** overall. This means that even though fewer jobs were added, the economy is not losing strength. Many companies are careful about hiring because of uncertainty in the economy. Still, unemployment rates stayed low, and wages continue to grow slowly. This steady job market gives some confidence to workers and businesses. Economists will watch future reports to see if this trend continues.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **weaker than expected** — 예상보다 약한  
>   _The sales this month were **weaker than expected**._  
> - **steady track** — 안정적인 흐름  
>   _The company’s profits are on a **steady track** this year._  
> - **labor market** — 노동 시장  
>   _The **labor market** is improving after the pandemic._  
> - **uncertainty in the economy** — 경제의 불확실성  
>   _Many businesses face **uncertainty in the economy** right now._  
> - **gives confidence** — 자신감을 준다

## 4. Silicon Valley Worried About Higher H-1B Visa Fees and Hiring Top Talent  
  

The US Department of Homeland Security has proposed a big increase in the fees for H-1B work visas. The new fee could reach over $100,000. This visa is very important for tech companies in Silicon Valley because it lets them hire skilled workers from other countries. Many tech leaders are asking, **"how will we hire top talent?"** if the fees become so high. They worry this change will make it harder to find the best workers. Some say it could slow down innovation and growth in the tech industry. The government will consider public feedback before making a final decision.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **propose an increase** — 인상안을 제안하다  
>   _The city council will **propose an increase** in taxes._  
> - **top talent** — 최고의 인재  
>   _The company always looks for **top talent** to join their team._  
> - **hire skilled workers** — 숙련된 노동자를 고용하다  
>   _Tech firms **hire skilled workers** from around the world._  
> - **make it harder** — 더 어렵게 만들다  
>   _New rules might **make it harder** to start a business._  
> - **slow down innovation** — 혁신을 늦추다  
>   _High costs can **slow down innovation** in technology._
