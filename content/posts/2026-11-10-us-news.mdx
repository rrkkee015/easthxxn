## 1. US Economy Adds 165,000 Jobs in October 

The US economy created 165,000 new jobs last month, showing a steady **job market** despite worries about the future. This number is a bit higher than experts expected. Many sectors, like health care and retail, helped to push the numbers up. The unemployment rate also stayed low, which means many people who want jobs can find work. This growth is important because it shows the economy is still **gaining ground** even with some economic uncertainty. However, some experts say the economy could slow down soon because of inflation and other challenges. Overall, this news is a positive sign for workers and businesses.

## 2. Trump Says Immigration Policy Was ‘Too Tough’ 

Former President Trump told the TV show *60 Minutes* that his immigration policy was **too tough** and it may have helped Democrats win the 2018 midterm elections. He said he is now open to working with Democrats to find a deal on immigration. Trump's policies focused on strict border control and deportations, which were popular with some voters but criticized by others. His new comments show a change in attitude, suggesting he might support a more flexible approach. This is important because immigration remains a major issue in US politics. It is not clear yet what kind of deal Trump would support or if Democrats will agree.

## 3. Investigators Find Voice Recorder from Deadly Florida Jet Crash 

Authorities have recovered the **voice recorder** from a small jet that crashed in Florida, killing nine people. The voice recorder helps investigators understand what happened before the crash. It records the sounds and conversations in the cockpit. Once investigators analyze the recorder, they can learn if technical problems or pilot errors caused the accident. This is an important step in the investigation. Families of the victims and the public want answers about the crash. The investigation will take time, but this discovery is a key development.

## 4. Silicon Valley Faces Major Changes After Trump Era 

Silicon Valley, the center of US technology companies, is going through a big change after the Trump years. Many companies are changing how they do business and how they work with the government. The old **playbook** or plan that helped Silicon Valley grow is now **falling apart**. New challenges include tougher government rules and competition from China. This shift could affect innovation and jobs in the tech industry. Experts say companies need to find new ways to succeed in this changing world. This story shows how politics and technology are closely connected.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **playbook** — 계획, 전략  
>   _The team followed the same **playbook** to win the game._  
> - **falling apart** — 무너지고 있는, 실패하고 있는  
>   _Their old business model is **falling apart** in today’s market._  
> - **major shift** — 큰 변화  
>   _The company is making a **major shift** to focus on renewable energy._
