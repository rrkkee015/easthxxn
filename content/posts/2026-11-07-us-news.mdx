## 1. New Immigration Policies Stir Controversy in Washington  


Recently, Stephen Miller, known for his strong views on immigration, was promoted to a top role in the White House. This move has caused **concern** among many industries that rely on immigrant workers. Miller is famous for creating tough immigration rules during the Trump administration. Many people worry that his return means stricter policies could return, which might make it harder for businesses to find workers from other countries. This is important because immigration affects many parts of the economy and society. The debate continues as lawmakers and companies watch closely to see what changes might come next. This situation shows how immigration remains a **hot topic** in US politics.

## 2. US Job Market Shows Strong Recovery With More Hiring  


The US labor market is improving faster than many expected. In October, job growth was strong, and the unemployment rate dropped to 4.1%. This means more people found work, which is a good sign for the economy. Experts say this growth helps families and businesses feel more confident about the future. However, some uncertainty remains because of global problems like inflation and supply chain issues. Still, the current data shows the job market is **regaining ground** after a tough period. This trend could continue if economic conditions stay stable. It is important to watch how these changes affect everyday life and money.

## 3. FBI Arrest Raises Concerns About Spies in US Tech Companies  


An FBI arrest has brought new attention to the idea that some foreign spies may work inside American tech companies. The arrest involved a person accused of stealing important information. This case is worrying because tech companies hold a lot of valuable data and secrets. Many experts say this event shows how important it is to keep good security. It also raises questions about how companies should protect themselves from foreign spying. This news comes at a time when technology is growing fast and plays a big role in daily life. People will be watching closely to see how the government and companies respond.

## 4. Tech Industry Faces New Political Challenges After Elections  


After recent elections, many tech leaders who supported Trump are now facing a changing environment. The US government may have new rules that affect how tech companies operate. Some experts say the tech industry has lost political influence in places like Silicon Valley. This shift could change how companies work with the government and how they handle issues like privacy and security. Democrats want to win back support from tech workers and leaders. The future may bring new partnerships or conflicts between politics and technology. This story shows how politics and tech are closely connected in America today.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **political influence** — 정치적 영향력  
>   _The company used its **political influence** to change the law._  
> - **win back** — 되찾다  
>   _The party hopes to **win back** voters in the tech sector._  
> - **partnership** — 협력 관계  
>   _A strong **partnership** between tech firms and government is important._
