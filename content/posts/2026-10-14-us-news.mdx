## 1. Concerns Rise Over Conditions at Delaney Hall Detention Center 

A congressman recently raised serious questions about the **conditions** at Delaney Hall, a detention center for immigrants. There have been deaths reported there, which makes the situation very worrying. The congressman asked officials to explain how they are keeping people safe and healthy in the center. This news is important because it shows concerns about how the U.S. treats immigrants in detention. Many advocates want better care and safer environments. The government might need to change policies or improve the facility soon. This story reminds us about the human side of immigration debates.

## 2. US Job Market Shows Steady Growth in September 

The U.S. labor market kept moving forward in September with steady hiring. Though the job growth was not very fast, it still shows the economy is holding up well. Many companies are still looking for workers, which is a good sign for people searching for jobs. This steady track means the economy is not facing big problems right now. Experts say this balance helps avoid big shocks or layoffs. It is also important because jobs affect many parts of people’s lives. We will watch if this steady trend continues in the coming months.

## 3. Silicon Valley’s AI Founders Support Industry Self-Policing on Safety 

In Silicon Valley, many AI company founders support former President Trump’s idea that the AI industry should **police itself** to ensure safety. This means the companies want to create their own rules and checks instead of waiting for the government. The founders believe they understand the technology best and can act faster. AI is growing quickly, and safety is a big concern because AI can affect many parts of life. Some experts agree but worry that without outside rules, problems might happen. This debate will likely continue as AI technology develops.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **police itself** — 스스로 감시하다, 스스로 규제하다  
>   _The community agreed to **police itself** to reduce crime._  
> - **create rules** — 규칙을 만들다  
>   _The team **created rules** for fair play._  
> - **act faster** — 더 빨리 행동하다  
>   _In emergencies, it is important to **act faster**._  
> - **safety concern** — 안전 문제  
>   _There is a **safety concern** about the new machine._  
> - **debate** — 토론, 논쟁  
>   _The class had a lively **debate** about climate change._

## 4. US-Korea Partnerships Are Key for Korea’s Tech Industry Growth 

The Korea Times reports that partnerships between the US and Korea are very important for Korea’s tech industry. These partnerships help Korea get new technology and share ideas. Many Korean companies work closely with US tech firms to develop better products. This cooperation is good for both countries because it creates jobs and supports innovation. Experts say keeping strong ties is vital as technology changes fast. The future of Korea’s tech depends a lot on these international relationships.
