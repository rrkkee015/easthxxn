## 1. UN Experts Demand Stop to Migrant Deportations to Third Countries 

The United Nations experts have called for an immediate **halt** to a controversial US policy that forces migrants to be deported to third countries. This policy makes it harder for many migrants to find safety and claim asylum in the United States. The experts say this practice harms vulnerable people, especially children, and violates international human rights laws. It is part of a broader crackdown on immigration under the current government. Many families are now stuck in dangerous situations while waiting for their cases to be reviewed. The experts urge the US to **reconsider** this policy and protect migrants’ basic rights. The future of this policy depends on possible legal challenges and political pressure ahead.

## 2. U.S. Job Market Shows Strong Growth With New Hiring Numbers 

In August, the United States added 162,000 new jobs, showing a strong **burst** of hiring activity. This growth helps reduce unemployment and supports the economy. However, it also puts more attention on **inflation**, which is the general rise in prices. When more people work and earn money, they spend more, which can increase inflation. Economists and policymakers are watching these numbers closely to decide future actions on interest rates. This hiring trend covers many industries and shows the resilience of the U.S. job market despite challenges. The data may influence how the government manages the economy in the coming months.

## 3. Silicon Valley Faces Challenges as H-1B Visa Fees Rise Sharply 

The US Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has proposed raising the fee for H-1B visas to an extremely high $103,265. This visa is very important for technology companies because it allows them to hire highly skilled workers from other countries. Many in Silicon Valley are worried and asking, **"how will we hire top talent?"** The fee increase could make it much harder and more expensive for companies to bring in skilled employees. This could slow down innovation and growth in the tech industry. The DHS says the fee hike is to cover the full cost of processing these visas. Now, the tech industry is watching closely as this policy could change how American companies compete globally.

## 4. America’s Tech Industry Expands Beyond Silicon Valley 

For many years, Silicon Valley was the **number one** tech hub in the United States. But now, other cities are growing fast and attracting many tech companies and startups. Places like Austin, Denver, and Miami are becoming popular for technology jobs and innovation. This change is because of lower costs, better quality of life, and new business opportunities in these cities. Experts say this spread of tech hubs could create more jobs and help new ideas develop across the country. It shows that the American tech scene is no longer focused in just one place. This shift may change where people choose to live and work in the future.
