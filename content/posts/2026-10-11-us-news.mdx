## 1. Tragic Shooting in Pennsylvania Shakes Community  


A sad event happened recently in Pennsylvania where a shooter killed 8 people at a family home before taking his own life. The police said this was a serious tragedy that shocked the local community. Incidents like this are rare but remind us how important it is to focus on safety and mental health. Authorities are still investigating what caused the shooting. Such events often lead to discussions about gun laws and support for affected families. This news is important because it affects many Americans and their sense of security. People are hoping for healing and solutions to prevent future violence.

## 2. Farmers Say Immigration Policies Raise Food Prices  


Farmers across the United States say that new immigration policies are making food more expensive. Many farms depend on immigrant workers to plant and harvest crops. When there are fewer workers, it costs more to grow food. This means prices at the grocery store can go up. Farmers worry that strict rules may hurt the food supply and cause problems for consumers. This story shows how immigration rules can affect more than just people’s lives — they can impact the whole economy. Policymakers may need to find a balance between security and supporting farmers.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **raise food prices** — 식품 가격을 올리다  
>   _Bad weather can **raise food prices** quickly._  
> - **depend on** — ~에 의존하다  
>   _Many businesses **depend on** seasonal workers._  
> - **cause problems for** — ~에 문제를 일으키다

## 3. US Job Market Shows Steady Growth Despite Slow Hiring  


In September, the United States added 29,000 new jobs, which is fewer than before, but the labor market is still strong. Experts say this slower hiring does not mean the economy is weak. Many companies are cautious because of global issues and inflation. However, job openings remain high, showing that many people want to work. The steady job market means workers still have good chances to find jobs. This news helps workers and businesses plan for the future. It also shows how the economy is slowly adjusting to new challenges.

They work together on new ideas and products, which helps both economies grow. This cooperation makes it easier to share technology and create better jobs. Experts say that strong partnerships like this help compete with other countries in the global market. The future looks bright if both continue to invest in research and development. This news shows how international teamwork in technology can bring many benefits.
