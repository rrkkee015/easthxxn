## 1. Xi Jinping’s Terms to Avoid US-China Military Conflict 

China’s leader Xi Jinping recently met with former US President Donald Trump. During this meeting, Xi explained the **terms** China wants to follow to prevent a military conflict between the US and China. He warned that both countries should be careful and avoid actions that could lead to a fight. This is important because the two countries are very powerful and a conflict could affect the whole world. Xi asked for respect for China’s interests and said dialogue is the best way to solve problems. Experts think this meeting shows both sides want to avoid war but tensions remain high. The future will depend on how well the US and China can **work together** on tricky issues like trade and security.

## 2. New Updates on US Immigration Authority 

The US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) announced changes in how immigration authority is shared with local law enforcement under Section 287(g) of the Immigration and Nationality Act. This program allows some local police officers to help enforce immigration laws. The goal is to improve cooperation between federal and local agencies to better control immigration issues. However, this program is also controversial because some people think it can lead to unfair treatment of immigrants. The government says the changes will make the program clearer and more effective. These changes could affect many communities where immigration is a hot topic.

>   _Local police have some **immigration authority** under the new rules._

## 3. US Job Market Shows Strong Growth in August 

In August, the United States added 162,000 new jobs, showing a strong **hiring burst** after slower months. This growth puts more attention on inflation because more jobs can mean higher spending and rising prices. Economists watch job numbers closely to understand the health of the economy. The increase surprised some experts who expected fewer jobs. This news may influence decisions by the Federal Reserve about interest rates. Workers and employers are hopeful, but they also watch for signs that the economy might slow down again.

## 4. Silicon Valley’s Tech Industry Fights New H-1B Fee Proposal 

The US tech industry group is asking the government to stop a new $103,265 fee proposal for H-1B visas. H-1B visas allow skilled foreign workers to work in the US tech sector. The tech companies say the high fee would hurt their ability to hire talented workers from other countries. They are worried this could slow down innovation and growth in Silicon Valley, the heart of the US tech scene. The group also asked how the government plans to support Silicon Valley if this fee goes forward. This issue is part of a bigger debate about immigration and the future of American technology.
