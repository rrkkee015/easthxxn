## 1. New York Names Special Prosecutor in Cornell Case 

New York Governor Kathy Hochul has **named** Letitia James as a special prosecutor in an important case involving Cornell University. This means Letitia James will **take charge** of the investigation to find out what happened. The case has drawn a lot of public attention because it involves serious questions about safety and responsibility. A special prosecutor is someone who works independently to make sure the investigation is fair and clear. This appointment shows that the government is **serious about** finding the truth. Letitia James has experience in handling tough legal cases, so many people expect her to do a good job. We will see what new information comes out as the investigation continues.

## 2. US Job Openings Drop, But Layoffs Stay Low 

In August, the number of job openings in the United States **fell** to 7.1 million. This means there are fewer new jobs available compared to before. However, layoffs are still **low**, which shows companies are not firing many workers. Experts say this could mean the job market is **starting to cool down** but is still strong. Many businesses are careful about hiring because of economic uncertainty. The unemployment rate is expected to stay about the same in September. This news is important because it helps people understand how easy or hard it may be to find a job soon.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **fell** — 떨어지다  
>   _The price of oil **fell** last week._  
> - **low** — 낮은  
>   _The number of accidents was **low** this year._  
> - **starting to cool down** — (시장 등이) 진정되기 시작하다  
>   _The housing market is **starting to cool down** after a boom._  
> - **economic uncertainty** — 경제적 불확실성  
>   _**Economic uncertainty** makes companies hesitant to invest._

## 3. Trump and AI CEOs Agree on Safety Pact for Artificial Intelligence 

Former President Trump and several top AI company leaders have **signed** a voluntary safety pact. This agreement shows their support for **developing AI** in a safe way. They also back plans to expand data centers, which are important for running AI systems. The pact means these companies promise to follow safety rules, but it is not a law. Experts hope this will help prevent problems with AI technology in the future. This is a big step because AI is growing fast and can affect many parts of life. The government and companies want to work together to **manage risks**.

## 4. Supreme Court to Review Trump’s Immigration Detention Policy 

The United States Supreme Court has decided to **review** a policy from the Trump administration about immigration detention. This policy requires many immigrants to stay in detention centers while waiting for their cases. Some people and groups argue this policy is unfair and should change. The Supreme Court will listen to arguments and make a final decision. This case is important because it affects many immigrants and the rules about how they are treated. The decision could change immigration practices across the country.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **review** — 검토하다, 다시 살피다  
>   _The manager will **review** the report before the meeting._  
> - **policy** — 정책  
>   _The school has a new **policy** on student attendance._  
> - **detention centers** — 구금 시설  
>   _Immigrants sometimes stay in **detention centers** while waiting for court decisions._  
> - **final decision** — 최종 결정
