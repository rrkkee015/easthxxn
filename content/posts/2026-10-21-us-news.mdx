## 1. Trump Holds Rally Despite GOP Cutting Campaign Funds  
  

Former President Donald Trump recently held a rally in North Carolina. This event happened even though the Republican Party decided to **pull its money** from his campaign. The GOP’s choice means they will stop giving Trump financial support for his race. Trump’s rally showed he still has support from many voters, but losing party money could make his efforts harder. This situation is important because it shows some tension inside the Republican Party. It might also affect how strong Trump’s campaign will be in the coming months before the elections. Many people are watching closely to see how this will change the political scene.

## 2. Richmond Mayor Proposes New ‘Trust Policy’ for Immigration  
  

The mayor of Richmond has introduced a draft for a new “trust policy.” This policy aims to improve relationships between local government and immigrant communities. It is designed to protect immigrants by limiting cooperation with federal immigration authorities. The goal is to create an environment where immigrants feel safe to report crimes and engage with local services. This policy is important because many immigrants fear deportation and avoid official help. If approved, Richmond could become a more welcoming city for immigrants. Other cities might follow Richmond’s example to build trust with immigrant populations.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **draft policy** — 정책 초안  
>   _The city council reviewed the **draft policy** before the final vote._  
> - **improve relationships** — 관계를 개선하다  
>   _Good communication can **improve relationships** between neighbors._  
> - **limit cooperation** — 협력을 제한하다

## 3. Amazon to Add 300,000 New US Jobs This Holiday Season  
  

Amazon announced it will create more than 300,000 new jobs in the United States this holiday season. These jobs come with higher pay and better benefits compared to last year. The company wants to meet the high demand for online shopping during holidays. Many of these positions will be temporary but could lead to permanent work. This news is good for people looking for jobs, especially in areas hit hard by unemployment. Amazon’s plan shows how big companies can **expand benefits** to attract workers. It also reflects the growing importance of e-commerce in the US economy.

## 4. FBI Arrest Sparks Concern About Spies in US Tech Companies  
  

Recently, the FBI arrested a person linked to spying in US technology firms. This case has increased worries that foreign spies may be trying to work inside American tech companies. Many tech companies are important for national security and hold valuable information. The arrest shows how serious the government is about protecting these companies. It also raises questions about how companies hire employees and check for security risks. Experts say this could lead to stricter rules for hiring in the tech industry. The story reminds us how technology and security are connected in today’s world.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **spark concern** — 우려를 불러일으키다  
>   _The new policy **sparked concern** among local residents._  
> - **linked to** — ~와 관련된  
>   _The suspect was **linked to** several thefts in the area._  
> - **national security** — 국가 안보  
>   _Protecting **national security** is a top priority for the government._  
> - **check for risks** — 위험을 확인하다  
>   _Companies must **check for risks** before signing contracts._  
> - **stricter rules** — 더 엄격한 규칙  
>   _Schools have introduced **stricter rules** to keep students safe._
