## 1. Trump Pushes Ahead with DC Golf Course Renovation Despite Court Order  


Former President Donald Trump announced plans to start renovating his golf course in Washington, DC. This move goes against a judge’s order that tries to stop the work. Trump says the renovation will bring jobs and improve the area. However, critics say it ignores the law and shows disrespect for the court system. The case may lead to more legal battles soon. This story shows how political conflicts can affect local projects and raise questions about following rules. People are watching closely to see if Trump will face consequences or change his plans.

## 2. Why Voting in Midterms Matters for Immigrants and Refugees  


The upcoming midterm elections are very important for immigrants and refugees in the United States. Many new laws and policies affecting immigration depend on who wins these elections. Groups working with immigrants say voting can change how refugees are treated and how easy it is to get legal status. They encourage everyone, especially immigrants, to participate in the voting process. This election may decide the future of immigration rules for years. Being involved in elections gives immigrants a chance to have their voices heard and protect their rights.

## 3. US Job Growth Slowed in September but Labor Market Remains Strong  


In September, the United States added fewer new jobs than before. The job growth slowed down sharply compared to previous months. Still, many jobs are available, and the labor market is described as **sturdy**. Experts say this means the economy is not in trouble yet, but it might be changing. The unemployment rate rose slightly to 4.2%. This small increase shows that some people are finding it harder to get work. The government and businesses will watch the job market closely to understand what comes next.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job growth** — 일자리 증가  
>   _The company announced strong **job growth** this quarter._  
> - **sturdy** — 튼튼한, 견고한  
>   _The labor market remains **sturdy** despite some challenges._  
> - **unemployment rate** — 실업률  
>   _The **unemployment rate** went up slightly last month._

## 4. FBI Arrest Raises Concerns About Spies in U.S. Tech Companies  


The FBI recently arrested a person connected to spying in the U.S. technology industry. This event has made people worried that foreign spies might be working inside American tech companies. The tech industry is very important for the country’s security and economy. Experts say companies need to be careful and watch for signs of spying. Some say this arrest shows a bigger problem that must be fixed soon. The government may increase efforts to protect technology secrets and stop spying activities.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **raise concerns** — 우려를 불러일으키다  
>   _The news of the arrest **raised concerns** about security._  
> - **spy** — 스파이, 간첩  
>   _The company fired an employee suspected of being a **spy**._  
> - **watch for** — 주의하다, 지켜보다  
>   _Employees should **watch for** unusual behavior._
