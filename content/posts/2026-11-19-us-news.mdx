## 1. Historic Meeting Planned Between Trump, Xi, and Putin 

Recently, three powerful leaders—Donald Trump, Xi Jinping, and Vladimir Putin—are **set to hold** a historic meeting. This meeting will happen on the sidelines of an important Asia-Pacific event called APEC. It is unusual because these leaders have many political differences and have not met together like this before. People are watching closely because this meeting could **shape** future global politics. Experts believe they will discuss trade, security, and world peace. This meeting might bring new agreements or increase tensions, depending on how the talks go. Many expect this to be a key moment for international relations.

## 2. Trump Administration Blocked from Ending Key Immigration Policy 

A recent court decision stopped the Trump administration from ending the “Duration of Status” immigration policy. This policy allows some immigrants to stay in the US legally while they study or work. The government wanted to end it, but the court said no. This means many immigrants can continue their studies or jobs without worry. The policy is important because it gives stability to thousands of people. Immigration groups say this is a big win. The government might try again to change immigration rules in the future, but for now, this policy remains in place.

Many big tech companies and leaders are starting to back these ideas. This change could **reshape** the tech industry’s future. Some believe it will affect how tech companies work with the government and handle issues like free speech and security. The shift shows that even technology is influenced by politics today.
