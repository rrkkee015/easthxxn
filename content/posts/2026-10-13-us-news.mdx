## 1. Trump Faces Strong Opposition Over Russian Diesel Import Deal  


The US President’s plan to import diesel fuel from Russia has met with **stiff bipartisan pushback**. This means both Democrats and Republicans are opposing the deal. The government wants to ease the energy shortage, but many worry this move could support Russia’s economy during a time of international conflict. Critics argue the deal sends the wrong message to allies and could harm US energy independence. Supporters say it could lower fuel prices and help consumers. The debate shows how energy policies can become political very quickly. We will see if the government changes its plan after this strong opposition.

## 4. US Job Growth Slows Sharply with Only 29,000 Added in September  


The US economy added only 29,000 jobs in September, showing the job market is losing its **spark**. This is much lower than expected. A slow job market can mean people find it harder to get new work. Many experts say this may be a sign that the economy is slowing down. However, some believe the job market is still **on a steady track** and not in trouble yet. Hiring will be important to watch in the next months to understand the trend. This news affects many workers and businesses across the country.
