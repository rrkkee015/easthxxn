## 1. Thanksgiving Storm Causes Flooding and Flight Delays Across the US  


A big storm hit the United States during Thanksgiving, causing heavy rain and flooding in many places. This storm made it hard for people to travel, especially by plane. Many flights were delayed or canceled, which caused long waits at airports. The flooding also damaged roads and homes in some areas, creating a difficult situation for families. Weather experts say this storm was stronger than usual for this time of year. It shows how important it is to be prepared for sudden weather changes. Officials are working to fix the damage and help people affected by the storm. Travelers are advised to check their flight status before going to the airport. This event reminds us how weather can **disrupt** our daily plans quickly.

> - **disrupt** — 방해하다, 혼란시키다  
>   _The storm **disrupted** many flights at the airport._  
> - **flight delays** — 항공편 지연  
>   _Due to bad weather, there were many **flight delays** yesterday._  
> - **heavy rain** — 폭우

> - **prepare for** — ~에 대비하다  
>   _It is important to **prepare for** severe weather._

## 2. States Are Changing Immigration Policies for 2026  


Many states in the U.S. are changing their immigration rules for the year 2026. Some states are making new laws to protect immigrants, while others are creating more restrictions. These changes can affect many people who live in the country without legal papers. Immigration policies are important because they decide who can stay and work in the U.S. The recent elections influenced these new rules a lot. Some states want to be more welcoming, while others want to control immigration more strictly. This situation shows how immigration is a big topic in American politics. People are watching closely to see how these policies will affect families and workers.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **immigration policy** — 이민 정책  
>   _The government is updating its **immigration policy** next year._  
> - **restrictions** — 제한  
>   _New **restrictions** make it harder for some immigrants to work._  
> - **protect** — 보호하다  
>   _Some states try to **protect** immigrant families._  
> - **influence** — 영향을 미치다

## 3. US Job Market Improves as Hiring Picks Up and Unemployment Falls  


The job market in the United States is getting stronger. More companies are hiring workers, and fewer people are unemployed. This is good news for the economy because when more people work, they can spend money and help businesses grow. The recent jobs report shows that many new jobs were created last month. Also, the unemployment rate dropped to its lowest point in a while. Experts say this improvement may continue if the economy stays stable. However, some challenges remain, like finding workers with the right skills. Overall, the job market is helping Americans feel more confident about their financial future.

## 4. Silicon Valley Sees Big Changes with New Tech Leadership  


Silicon Valley, the center of technology in the U.S., is going through important changes. A new leader, Jensen Huang, returned to head Nvidia, a top tech company. His return came after strong support from the tech industry and lobby groups. This change shows how powerful tech companies are in shaping the future of technology and business. Nvidia is known for making computer chips used in many devices, like gaming consoles and self-driving cars. Experts believe Huang’s leadership will help the company grow even more. This could influence the whole tech landscape, including how new products are made and how the industry works with the government.

> ### 영어 표현 학습

> - **leadership** — 리더십, 지도력
