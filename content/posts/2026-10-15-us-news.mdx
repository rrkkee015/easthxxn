## 1. Texas Senate Race Lead Shrinks as Election Nears 

The race for the Texas Senate seat is getting closer. Recent polls show that Talarico is still **ahead**, but his **lead has narrowed** compared to before. This means the other candidates are catching up and the election could be very competitive. Texas is an important state, so this race is being watched closely by many people. The results will affect the balance of power in the U.S. Senate. Voters are paying attention to issues like the economy and immigration. The next few weeks will be important as candidates try to **win over** more voters before Election Day.

## 2. Immigration Policy Changes Affect Virginia’s Workforce 

Virginia’s employers and workers are feeling the effects of new immigration policies. Changes in immigration rules can **shape** the workforce by affecting who can work legally in the state. Many businesses rely on immigrant workers for important jobs. When policies become stricter, companies may have trouble finding enough employees. This can slow down growth and cause problems for the local economy. Some leaders say the right policies should help both workers and employers. Understanding these changes is important because they **impact** many people’s lives.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **shape** — 형성하다; 영향을 주다  
>   _Immigration policies **shape** the future of the workforce._  
> - **impact** — 영향  
>   _New laws can **impact** many businesses._  
> - **rely on** — ~에 의존하다  
>   _Many farms **rely on** immigrant workers._

## 3. AI Automates Coding, But Developer Jobs Still Grow 

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is now helping to write computer code automatically. This might make people think there will be fewer jobs for software developers. However, job postings for developers are actually increasing. This shows that AI is creating new opportunities instead of replacing workers. Companies still need skilled developers to manage and improve AI tools. The rise of AI means the tech job market is changing fast. Developers who learn to work with AI could have an advantage in the future.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **automate** — 자동화하다  
>   _AI can **automate** many tasks to save time._  
> - **job postings** — 구인 광고  
>   _There are more **job postings** for software developers now._  
> - **opportunities** — 기회  
>   _New technology creates **opportunities** for workers._

## 4. Silicon Valley Supports AI Industry Self-Policing on Safety 

In Silicon Valley, many AI company founders support President Trump’s idea that the AI industry should **police itself**. This means companies would set their own rules and safety guidelines without much government control. They believe this approach can lead to faster and better innovation. But some experts worry that without outside checks, companies might miss important risks. AI technology is growing very fast, so safety is a big concern. The debate continues on how to balance innovation with protecting people from AI problems.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **police itself** — 스스로 규제하다  
>   _The industry wants to **police itself** to stay flexible._  
> - **innovation** — 혁신  
>   _New ideas drive **innovation** in technology._  
> - **safety guidelines** — 안전 지침  
>   _Companies follow **safety guidelines** to protect users._
