The US Army announced it must safely **detonate** a dangerous chemical called lead azide in Iowa. This chemical is very sensitive and can explode easily. This event shows how important it is to handle explosives carefully. Local officials are helping with the evacuation plans. After the detonation, people will be allowed to return home. ## 2. California Passes New Laws to Restrict Federal Immigration Enforcement 

California’s Democratic leaders passed new laws that limit how federal immigration officers work in the state. These laws create more protections for immigrants by restricting federal agents’ power. This change is part of a larger national debate about immigration policy. California wants to keep immigrants safe and reduce fear in immigrant communities. However, some people believe these laws make it harder to enforce immigration rules. The new laws may cause more legal fights between the state and federal government. This shows how immigration remains a key issue in US politics.

## 3. US Job Market Shows Mixed Signals with Layoffs and New Hiring 

Kroger, a large US grocery chain, is cutting about 3,000 corporate jobs in the United States. However, at the same time, Kroger is hiring many workers in India. This shows how companies are changing where they get help from. Meanwhile, US job numbers are still strong. In October, 165,000 new jobs were added across the country, which was better than many experts expected. Despite this, the prices of goods are rising, and many people feel that their money does not buy as much as before. The job market looks good, but inflation is a concern for many Americans.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **cut jobs** — 일자리를 줄이다

> - **hire workers** — 직원을 고용하다  
>   _They will **hire workers** for the new store._

> - **inflation** — 물가 상승

## 4. Silicon Valley Faces Challenges Over Chinese Tech Competition 

Silicon Valley, the US center for technology and innovation, is facing new problems because of advances in Chinese technology. Some American companies want to stop Chinese access to US artificial intelligence (AI) tools. This has caused a split in Silicon Valley between those who support strict controls and those who do not. The US government is also worried about China’s rapid tech growth. This situation could change how technology companies work with China in the future. It shows how global politics affect the technology industry.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **tech advances** — 기술 발전  
>   _China’s **tech advances** are growing quickly._  
> - **artificial intelligence (AI)** — 인공지능  
>   _Many companies use **artificial intelligence** to improve services._  
> - **strict controls** — 엄격한 통제  
>   _The government wants **strict controls** on data privacy._  
> - **split** — 분열, 의견 차이
