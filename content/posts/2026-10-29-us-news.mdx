## 1. Judge Allows California Ballots to Be Processed Despite Justice Department Objection  


A judge recently said no to the Justice Department’s request to **stop processing** ballots in California. This means that the state can keep counting votes for now. The Justice Department wanted to pause the ballot counting because of some concerns, but the judge did not agree. This is important because the vote count affects election results and public trust. California has many voters, so delays or stops in counting could cause problems. The judge’s decision lets the election process continue without interruption. It shows how courts can play a key role in protecting voting rights during elections.

## 2. How Trump Changed Immigration Rules in the US  


Since President Trump took office, he made many changes to immigration policies in the US. These changes included stricter rules for people who want to enter the country. Some of the new rules made it harder for immigrants to get visas or stay legally. These changes have caused worry among many people, especially Latino Republicans in places like Maryland. Immigration is a big topic in US politics, especially before elections. Some experts say these strict policies may cause a **backlash** — meaning people might vote against politicians who support these rules. The future of immigration laws could change after the midterm elections.

## 3. U.S. Manufacturing Jobs Are Growing, But Hiring Is Different Now  


Manufacturing in the US is adding jobs again after some slow months. However, companies are now following new **hiring rules**. These rules mean that the way factories find and keep workers has changed. For example, there may be more focus on safety or new skills. The job market is not the same as before the pandemic. Workers also expect different things from their jobs now. This trend affects the economy because manufacturing is a big part of it. Experts will watch how these changes affect job growth in the future.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **adding jobs** — 일자리를 늘리다  
>   _The company is **adding jobs** to meet growing demand._  
> - **hiring rules** — 채용 규칙  
>   _The new **hiring rules** require background checks for all applicants._  
> - **job market** — 취업 시장

> - **focus on** — 집중하다

> - **affect the economy** — 경제에 영향을 미치다

## 4. Anthropic’s IPO Could Have a Big “Trickle-Down Effect” on Tech  


Anthropic, an artificial intelligence company, is planning to have an IPO, which means selling shares to the public for the first time. This event is expected to have a **trickle-down effect** in both Wall Street and Silicon Valley. A **trickle-down effect** means that when a big company does well, it helps other smaller companies and people too. Investors are watching closely because AI technology is growing fast. This IPO could change how people invest in tech and influence new AI projects. It shows how important tech companies have become in the economy.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **IPO** — 기업공개 (주식을 처음으로 일반에 파는 것)  
>   _The startup announced its **IPO** next month._  
> - **trickle-down effect** — 파급 효과  
>   _The new policy had a **trickle-down effect** on small businesses._  
> - **investors** — 투자자들  
>   _**Investors** are interested in companies with strong growth._  
> - **growing fast** — 빠르게 성장하는  
>   _The tech industry is **growing fast** this year._  
> - **influence** — 영향을 주다  
>   _Social media can **influence** people’s opinions._
