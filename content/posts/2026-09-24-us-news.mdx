## 1. Trump Welcomes China’s Xi Jinping at Rare Airport Summit  


Former President Donald Trump gave Chinese leader Xi Jinping a **rare airport welcome** as Xi arrived for a special summit. This kind of greeting is unusual and shows the importance of the meeting. The two leaders will discuss key issues like trade and security between the US and China. This summit comes at a time when relations have been tense. Many people are watching closely to see if this meeting will lead to better cooperation or more conflict. The event is important because China and the US have a big influence on the world’s economy and peace. Experts say the way Trump greeted Xi shows a mix of respect and political strategy.

## 2. Wisconsin’s Caregiver Shortage Worsened by Immigration Policy  


Leaders in Wisconsin say that the state’s shortage of caregivers is getting worse because of federal immigration policies. Caregivers help elderly and disabled people with daily tasks. Many caregivers in Wisconsin are immigrants. New rules make it harder for some immigrants to stay and work in the state. This causes fewer workers to be available, which hurts people who need care. The shortage is a big problem because the population is aging and more care is needed. Officials hope the government will change policies to help bring in more caregivers soon.

## 3. U.S. Adds 162,000 Jobs in August, But Inflation Still a Concern  


The U.S. economy added 162,000 new jobs in August, showing a strong hiring **burst**. This is good news for workers looking for jobs. However, many people are worried because wages are not growing fast enough to keep up with inflation. Inflation means prices for things like food and gas are going up. If wages don’t increase too, people have less money to spend. Experts say the government and businesses will watch these numbers carefully. The job growth could help the economy, but the fight against inflation is still important.

> ### 영어 표현 학습

>   _**Inflation** makes everyday items more expensive._

## 4. Silicon Valley’s New Two-Year Tech Academy Aims to Train Future Talent  


Andreessen Horowitz, a big investment company in Silicon Valley, is starting a two-year tech academy. This academy will offer an alternative to college for people who want to work in technology. The program focuses on teaching practical skills that employers want. It is designed to help students get ready for jobs in software, engineering, and other tech fields. Many tech companies support this idea because they need more skilled workers. This new academy could change how people prepare for tech careers and help solve the talent shortage in the industry.
