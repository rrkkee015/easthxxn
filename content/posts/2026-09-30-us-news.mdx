## 1. Judge Halts New York’s Tax Plan on Second Homes 

A judge has ordered New York to **scrap** its new tax plan on second homes. The plan was meant to tax owners of vacation houses more, but the judge said it was not done properly. Now, New York must **start over** and create a new plan that follows the rules. This decision is important because the tax could affect many people who own homes in the state. The government wanted to raise money to help pay for services, but the court found problems with how the tax was designed. The state will likely work on a new plan that will be fairer and legal. Many homeowners and officials are waiting to see what comes next. This case shows how courts can influence government policies.

## 2. California Governor Fights Against Harsh Immigration Tactics 

Governor Newsom of California signed new bills to **push back on** tough immigration tactics used by the Trump administration. These tactics were called “Orwellian,” meaning they were seen as unfair and strict. The new laws aim to **protect immigrant communities** by limiting how much immigration officers can act inside the state. This is important because many people feel worried about their safety and rights. California wants to be a safe place for all people, no matter where they come from. The governor’s actions may cause more states to follow and fight for immigrants’ rights. This story shows how local governments can respond to federal policies.

## 3. US Job Openings Fall but Labor Market Stays Strong 

In August, the number of US job openings fell to 7.1 million, which is lower than before. However, experts say the labor market is still **sturdy** and healthy. This means many people can still find jobs even if there are fewer openings. Layoffs remain low, so companies are not firing many workers. The job market is important because it shows how the economy is doing. Some say hiring is slower, but overall, the situation is stable. Workers and employers are watching closely to see if things will improve or get worse. This news helps people understand the current job situation in America.

## 4. Silicon Valley Faces Challenges with New H-1B Fee Proposal 

The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has proposed to **increase** the H-1B visa fee to $103,265, a huge rise from before. This visa is very important for tech companies to **hire top talent** from other countries. Silicon Valley leaders are worried and asking, “**How will we hire top talent** if the fees get so high?” The new fee could make it harder and more expensive for companies to bring skilled workers. This may affect innovation and growth in the U.S. tech industry. Many are waiting to see if the government will change this plan after the public talks. This shows the struggle between immigration policy and business needs.
