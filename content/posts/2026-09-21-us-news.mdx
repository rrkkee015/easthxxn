## 1. ICE Officer Shoots and Wounds Man in Austin, Texas 

An ICE officer shot and wounded a man in Austin, Texas, according to officials. The incident happened during a law enforcement operation. Authorities said the man was injured but survived. This shooting has raised questions about the use of force by immigration officers. People in Austin and across the country are watching closely. The case may lead to more discussions about police and immigration enforcement practices. Officials promise to investigate what happened fully and fairly. This story is important because it shows the challenges in immigration law enforcement today.

## 2. U.S. Adds 162,000 Jobs in August, Surpassing Expectations 

The United States added 162,000 new jobs in August, which is much more than experts expected. This shows that the job market is still strong. The unemployment rate stayed low at 4.1 percent. However, wage growth is slower than inflation, which means people’s pay is not keeping up with rising prices. This could affect how Americans spend money in the future. The strong job growth may also influence decisions about inflation and interest rates. Economists will watch the next reports closely to see if this trend continues.

> - **job market** — 취업 시장  
>   _The **job market** is improving this year._  
> - **unemployment rate** — 실업률  
>   _The **unemployment rate** dropped last month._  
> - **wage growth** — 임금 상승  
>   _Slow **wage growth** can make it hard to keep up with expenses._  
> - **keep up with** — 따라가다, 뒤처지지 않다  
>   _Her salary did not **keep up with** inflation._

It also means Silicon Valley is no longer the only important spot for technology. Factors like lower costs and local support encourage this change. The future may see a more balanced tech economy with many strong regions.

## 4. Trump’s Immigration Policy Reflects 1920s Attitudes 

Former President Trump’s immigration policies remind some people of the 1920s. Back then, the U.S. limited certain groups from entering the country. Trump’s rules also focused on restricting newcomers from specific places. This has caused debates about fairness and the country’s values. Some argue that such policies hurt families and the economy. Others say they protect national security. The history behind these policies helps us understand today’s immigration discussions better.
