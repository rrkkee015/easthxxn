## 1. Powerful Nor’easter Brings Heavy Rain and Flooding to Northeast U.S.  
  

A strong nor’easter storm hit the Northeast part of the United States recently. This storm brought **heavy rain** and strong winds. Many coastal areas saw flooding, which caused problems for local residents and traffic. People were told to stay safe and avoid driving in dangerous conditions. This type of storm can cause damage to homes and businesses near the coast. Weather experts say such storms may become more common and stronger in the future. Officials are working to help people prepare for more bad weather like this.

## 2. Caregiver Shortage in Wisconsin Linked to Immigration Policy  
  

Wisconsin is facing a big problem: not enough caregivers to help elderly and disabled people. Leaders say that federal immigration rules may be making this shortage worse. Many caregivers in the state come from other countries, and new policies make it harder for them to work legally in the U.S. This shortage means fewer people get the help they need at home or in care centers. Officials hope to find better immigration solutions to fix the problem. If the policy changes, it could improve the jobs situation and care for vulnerable people.

## 3. U.S. Job Market Shows Strong Growth with 162,000 Jobs Added in August  
  

The U.S. added 162,000 new jobs in August, which was more than many experts expected. This is good news for workers and the economy. The unemployment rate stayed low at 4.1 percent. Many people are finding work again after the problems caused by the pandemic. This strong job growth may affect other things like inflation and interest rates. Economists will watch these numbers closely to see what happens next. For now, the job market looks healthier than some had feared.

## 4. Silicon Valley Worried About New H-1B Visa Fee Increase  
  

The U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has proposed a big increase in the fee for H-1B work visas. This fee could go up to $103,265, which is much higher than before. Many companies in Silicon Valley say this will make it harder to **hire top talent** from other countries. The H-1B visa helps tech firms bring skilled workers from abroad. If the fee goes up, some companies may not afford to hire these workers. Experts warn this could slow down innovation and growth in the tech industry. The government will review feedback before making a final decision.

> - **hire top talent** — 최고의 인재를 채용하다  
>   _Tech companies want to **hire top talent** from around the world._  
> - **fee increase** — 요금 인상  
>   _The **fee increase** may affect many businesses._  
> - **skilled workers** — 숙련된 노동자  
>   _The H-1B visa allows companies to hire **skilled workers**._  
> - **slow down** — 늦추다, 지연시키다
